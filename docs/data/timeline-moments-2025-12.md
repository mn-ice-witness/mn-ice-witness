---
date: 2025-12-01
title: Operation Metro Surge Launched
incident: 2025-12-01-operation-metro-surge-launched
source:
---
DHS announces "Operation Metro Surge," deploying over 100 federal ICE agents to the Minneapolis-St. Paul metropolitan area — the first time a single U.S. metro has been the focus of a dedicated federal immigration surge. DHS calls targets the "worst of the worst"; local attorneys immediately dispute the characterization.

---
date: 2025-12-09
title: U.S. Citizen Tackled, Detained for "Looking Somali"
incident: 2025-12-09-mubashir-wrongful-detention
source:
---
The first major wrongful detention of a U.S. citizen. 20-year-old Mubashir Hussen — a naturalized Somali-American who came to the U.S. at age 1 — is tackled and placed in a chokehold by ICE agents in Cedar-Riverside despite offering to show his passport. Mayor Jacob Frey condemns the arrest, establishing the pattern of racial profiling that would define the operation. For a list of all U.S. citizen and legal resident incidents, see [list/citizens](/list/citizens).

---
date: 2025-12-07
title: Guns Pointed at Elderly Couple Observing ICE
incident: 2025-12-07-biestman-lee-guns-pointed
source:
---
John Biestman and his wife Janet Lee follow an ICE vehicle near Church of the Assumption in Richfield. Four unmarked cars box them in; agents point semiautomatic weapons in their faces. Biestman: "This is like Germany 1938." Across the street, a woman photographing the encounter also has weapons pointed at her. When a Richfield officer arrives, an agent tells her: "See what happens when you call the police? Nothing. They can't do shit to us." The couple become named plaintiffs in the ACLU's [federal lawsuit](/entry/2025-12-17-tincher-v-noem-aclu-lawsuit). For a list of all observer incidents, see [list/observers](/list/observers).

---
date: 2025-12-15
title: Pregnant Somali Woman Dragged Through Snow at Karmel Mall
incident: 2025-12-15-karmel-mall-pregnant-woman
source:
---
The first nationally viral moment. ICE agents arrive at Karmel Mall — the nation's first Somali shopping center — and detain at least four people. A pregnant woman is dragged through the snow; video goes viral on TMZ and national outlets. During the same confrontation, [Abdikadir Noor](/entry/2025-12-16-abdikadir-noor-karmel-mall), a U.S. citizen trying to calm the crowd, is tackled and arrested. The mall falls silent in the weeks that follow.

---
date: 2025-12-17
title: ACLU Files First Lawsuit Challenging Treatment of Observers
incident: 2025-12-17-tincher-v-noem-aclu-lawsuit
source:
---
The ACLU of Minnesota files [Tincher v. Noem](/entry/2025-12-17-tincher-v-noem-aclu-lawsuit), producing the only preliminary injunction against any aspect of Operation Metro Surge — barring agents from retaliating against, pepper spraying, or stopping people for watching them. The Eighth Circuit stays the order 10 days later, but the evidentiary record — more than 30 sworn declarations — exists permanently.

---
date: 2025-12-31
title: Patient Shackled to Hospital Bed; DHS Retaliates Against Hospital
incident: 2025-12-31-hcmc-patient-shackled
source:
---
ICE agents enter Hennepin County Medical Center without a judicial warrant and shackle a patient to his hospital bed for 28 hours. After county commissioners confirm no warrant exists, agents are ordered to leave. Days later, DHS [subpoenas I-9 employment records](/entry/2026-01-08-hennepin-healthcare-i9-subpoena) for more than 7,000 Hennepin Healthcare employees — a move Commissioner Angela Conley calls "retaliatory." In the weeks ahead, a [man is detained driving his sick wife to the ER](/entry/2026-01-16-parral-ortiz-hospital-detention) and a [Libyan man seeking treatment is shackled despite a painful skin condition](/entry/2026-01-11-hani-duglof-medical), breaking a long-standing precedent against immigration enforcement at hospitals. For a list of all hospital and school incidents, see [list/schools-hospitals](/list/schools-hospitals).
