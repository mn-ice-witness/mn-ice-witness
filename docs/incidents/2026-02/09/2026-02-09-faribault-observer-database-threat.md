# ICE Agents Tell Observer They Are Building "Database" of Those Documenting Operations

## Summary
A U.S. citizen was stopped by ICE agents in Faribault on Monday, February 9. Agents called her by name and told her they were creating a "database" of those organizing to document the federal operation in Minnesota. She was released after the stop. Separately, Gina Washburn, a volunteer with Northfield Supporting Neighbors, reported that an agent she spoke with at the Flying J travel center the previous month also knew her by name and took her photograph. DHS has not responded to questions about the alleged database.
