# Vehicle Crashes Near Robbinsdale City Hall During ICE Pursuit

## Summary

Federal agents pursued a vehicle near Robbinsdale City Hall on February 5 in an attempt to arrest a passenger. The driver, a 36-year-old man, crashed into a retaining wall near the rain garden southeast of City Hall. A 36-year-old woman passenger was found inside City Hall experiencing severe anxiety with minor injuries; she declined medical care and was given a ride home. Robbinsdale Police stated they had no prior knowledge of the operation and do not cooperate with ICE agents.

### Robbinsdale Police Department

Captain John Elder clarified that the woman was the passenger, not the driver as initially reported: "Had she been the driver, we could've charged her with leaving the scene of the crash."

Robbinsdale PD stated: "We had no knowledge of this prior to arriving on scene. The Robbinsdale Police Department does not cooperate with ICE agents."
