# Kidney Transplant Recipient Arrested in Rochester Needs Anti-Rejection Medication

## Summary
Federal immigration agents arrested Javier Abreu, 38, on Civic Center Drive in Rochester on February 5 while he was delivering groceries for his church. Agents rammed his car and broke his window during the arrest. Abreu is a kidney transplant recipient who requires anti-rejection medication, and State Rep. Kim Hicks says his access to the medication has been inconsistent. He was transported to the Whipple Federal Building near Ft. Snelling and was scheduled to be flown to a facility in Texas on February 6.

## Official Accounts

### Federal Response
DHS did not respond to MPR News' request for comment. Federal officials gave conflicting information about Abreu's medication access — initially requiring a doctor's note, then indicating he was already receiving medication.

### Local Officials
**Rep. Kim Hicks (DFL-Rochester):**
> "I don't understand why the federal government is taking Minnesotans who are not criminals, who are part of our communities, and violently ripping them off the streets after running into their cars, and then denying them access to life saving medication."

> "It's a game of telephone."

> "I do not understand how picking up law-abiding immigrants who are following the process benefits anyone. I don't understand how ramming a car and breaking their window benefits anyone. This doesn't align with Minnesota values or the Constitution."

Hicks described herself as a friend of Abreu's family.
