# Two Detained by ICE Inside Hennepin County Courthouse After Hearing

## Summary

Federal immigration agents detained two individuals inside the Hennepin County Government Center on February 5 near the security screening area, after a court hearing on criminal sexual conduct charges. Sheriff's deputies aided the detention. Reports indicate a woman was dragged off a Government Center elevator and handed to ICE agents. Chief Public Defender Mike Berger called the arrests unconstitutional, stating detainees were denied access to their attorneys. County Attorney Mary Moriarty said ICE is "degrading public safety by detaining individuals who have not been convicted."

## Affected Individual(s)

- **Daniel Alejandro Torrealba Mendez:** Awaiting trial on criminal sexual conduct charges
- **Joseelin Josimar Castillo Sequera:** Charged with aiding and abetting

## What Happened

ICE agents detained both individuals near the courtroom security screening area inside the Government Center towers after their court hearing. Sheriff's deputies were present and aided the detention. According to WCCO Radio, a woman was also dragged off a Government Center elevator and handed to ICE agents.

The Fourth Judicial District stated that federal agents can enter the courthouse to detain people.

## Official Statements

**Chief Public Defender Mike Berger:** Called the arrests unconstitutional. Stated the detainees were not allowed to speak with their attorneys. Described ICE arrests before completion of the state case as "a new and unprecedented interruption of due process in state courts."

**County Attorney Mary Moriarty:** Called the detentions harmful to the victim and said ICE is "degrading public safety by detaining individuals who have not been convicted."
