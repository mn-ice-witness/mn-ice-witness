# 11 Federal Agents Smash Into Apartment Building at 6 a.m. to Arrest Man for Anti-ICE Social Media Posts

## Summary
At approximately 6 a.m. on February 5, 2026, at least 11 Homeland Security Investigations officers in camouflage and masks broke through the security window of Eat Street Flats — a residential apartment building in Minneapolis's Whittier neighborhood — to arrest 37-year-old Kyle Wagner. Rather than contact the building's property manager or use the front door, agents smashed the glass beside the security entrance, then broke down Wagner's apartment door upstairs. Wagner was charged with cyberstalking and interstate communications out of the U.S. Attorney's Office in Eastern Michigan for allegedly doxxing a pro-ICE individual and making threatening social media posts against ICE agents. The broken security window left the building's entrance unsecured for all residents.

## Timeline
- **~6:00 a.m., Feb 5** — HSI officers break through the security window next to the side entrance of Eat Street Flats (not the front door). At least 11 officers in camouflage and masks participate.
- **Entry** — After breaking through the window, two officers in military gear walk upstairs. Shards of glass are still falling. Two more officers approach and reach through the broken window to open the door from inside.
- **Apartment breached** — Officers break down the door of Wagner's unit.
- **Arrest** — Wagner is led out of the building. Photos show him wearing a sweatshirt reading "I'M ANTIFA!"
- **Aftermath** — Wagner's dog is left behind in the unit. A neighbor takes in the animal. Contractors board up the building and clean up broken glass.
- **Criminal complaint** — Complaint was filed Tuesday (Feb 3) in Eastern Michigan and unsealed Thursday (Feb 5).
