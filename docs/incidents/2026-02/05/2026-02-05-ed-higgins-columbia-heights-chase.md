# Observer Chased by Federal Agents Through Columbia Heights, Cornered at Police Station

## Summary
Ed Higgins, a 48-year-old Marine veteran and community observer, drove past Sunnyside Mobile Home Park on Feb. 5 to check on reported ICE presence and honked his horn to warn residents. Federal agents pursued his van for over a mile down Central Avenue, repeatedly attempting to force him off the road. Higgins called 911 during the chase, telling dispatchers agents were "trying to run into me right now." He drove to the Columbia Heights Police Department for safety, where surveillance video shows agents boxing in his van, drawing firearms, and striking his car windows. BCA Deputy Superintendent Scott Mueller, who happened to be in the parking lot, intervened and de-escalated the situation. Higgins was taken to the Whipple Federal Building and released the same day without charges.
