# ICE Removes Mental Health Patient from Edina Hospital, Transfers to Texas

## Summary
ICE agents arrested Adrian Sotelo Guzman on Feb. 19 during Operation Metro Surge and brought him to M Health Fairview Southdale Hospital in Edina after he exhibited "abnormal behavior and an altered mental state." He was diagnosed with bipolar disorder with psychotic features and hospitalized, while ICE agents camped in or near the emergency department for a week. Despite a habeas corpus petition and an initial court order blocking his transfer, Judge Daniel Traynor reversed course and allowed ICE to remove him to Texas. Attorneys initially located him at Camp East Montana, a tent camp in El Paso, before confirming he was moved to El Paso Behavioral Health System. His family and Unidos MN have called for an investigation into the discharge.
