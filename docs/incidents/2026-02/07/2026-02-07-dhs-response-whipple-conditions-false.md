# DHS Statement: Claims of "Subprime Conditions" at Whipple Are "FALSE"

## Summary
On February 7, 2026, DHS issued a statement pushing back on Rep. Kelly Morrison's documented observations of poor conditions at the Bishop Henry Whipple Federal Building, declaring: "Any claim that there are subprime conditions at ICE facilities are FALSE." The statement came one day after Morrison — a physician with over 20 years of medical experience — toured the facility and reported detainees in leg shackles on concrete floors with no beds, thin foil blankets, no posted medical protocols, and minimal food. DHS described the facility as a temporary processing space "operated strictly in accordance with all federal regulations and policies." DHS spokesperson Tricia McLaughlin called Morrison's characterization "categorically false."

## The Statement

DHS posted on X (@DHSgov) on February 7:

> FAKE NEWS. Any claim that there are subprime conditions at ICE detention facilities are FALSE. All detainees are provided with proper meals, quality water, blankets, medical treatment, and have opportunities to communicate with their family members and lawyers. ICE has higher detention standards than most U.S. prisons that hold actual U.S. citizens.

DHS described the Whipple Building as a temporary processing facility:

> The ICE Saint Paul Field Office is located within the Bishop Henry Whipple Federal Building and has holding rooms for processing arrests, it is not a detention center. The holding rooms within the facility are temporary spaces used only while individuals are being processed and awaiting transfer to a long-term detention facility. They are operated strictly in accordance with all federal regulations and policies.

DHS spokesperson Tricia McLaughlin told reporters: "Any claim that there are subprime conditions at ICE facilities is categorically false. All detainees are provided with proper meals, medical treatment, and have opportunities to communicate with their family members and lawyers."

## DHS Claims vs. Morrison's Documented Observations

Morrison, a physician, toured the Whipple facility multiple times between February 1 and February 6, 2026.

| Issue | Morrison Reported | DHS Claimed |
|-------|------------------|-------------|
| **Sleeping** | Concrete floors, no beds, thin foil blankets | "Blankets" provided |
| **Medical care** | No posted medical protocols, no nurse during some visits, no measles prevention plan | "Medical treatment" provided |
| **Food** | Minimal food | "Proper meals" provided |
| **Leg shackles** | Detainees in leg shackles at all times | No response |
| **Facility purpose** | Functioning as detention facility | "Not a detention center" — temporary processing space |
| **Overall** | "Unacceptable," called for facility to be "shut down" | "Operated strictly in accordance with all federal regulations and policies" |

Morrison stated: "People are sleeping on concrete floors, they don't have real blankets, they're staying on those temporary tin foil blankets." She described conditions as "very dehumanizing" and "beneath what we should expect in the United States of America."

DHS's mention of "blankets" in the February 7 tweet — which was not included in their earlier standard denial language — appeared to be a direct response to Morrison's observation about foil blankets.

## Context

The DHS statement followed weeks of mounting documentation of conditions at Whipple:

- **Jan 31** - Star Tribune published detainee accounts describing overcrowded cells, overflowing toilets, and being held in shackles on concrete floors.
- **Feb 1** - Morrison's first visit; she called conditions "unacceptable."
- **Feb 4** - Border Czar Tom Homan stated "the conditions looked fine to me yesterday."
- **Feb 5-6** - Morrison toured the facility additional times, calling for it to be "shut down."
- **Feb 6** - U.S. District Judge Nancy Brasel told DHS the gap between evidence and government claims was "so enormous I don't know how you're going to close it."

Reps. Angie Craig and Betty McCollum, who were denied entry on the same day Morrison toured, responded: "We have heard countless reports that detainees are being held in unlivable conditions at Whipple. We have every reason to believe that this administration is once again lying through their teeth."
