# Honduran Mother with Brain Surgery History Held Extra Days Despite Federal Release Order; Attorney Cannot Find Her

## Summary
A Honduran mother of two with a pending asylum case, valid work permit, and recent brain surgery was taken into federal custody during Operation Metro Surge. Despite a federal release order, attorney Danielle Robinson Briand of Justicias Law was repeatedly denied access to her client and could not locate her in the immigration custody system for several days. Upon eventual release, ICE retained the woman's work permit and social security card.
