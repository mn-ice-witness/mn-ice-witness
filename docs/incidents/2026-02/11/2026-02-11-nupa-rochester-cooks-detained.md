# Two Cooks Detained in Nupa Restaurant Parking Lot; Rochester Location Forced to Close

## Summary
ICE agents detained two brothers working as cooks at Nupa Mediterranean Grill in their parking lot as they arrived for their shift. The restaurant owner said the brothers had no criminal records and were paying taxes, Social Security, and Medicare. ICE never asked for documentation before arresting them. One Nupa location closed temporarily; one brother was later released after an immigration hearing.

## Official Accounts

### Restaurant Owner
Ted Paizis: "You lose a couple employees, it really affects the operation. They became part of our family." He noted the brothers "were working, paying their taxes, paid Social Security, Medicare. No criminal records that we know of." The restaurant said ICE never asked for documentation before making the arrests.

### Local Officials
Rochester Mayor Kim Norton said the detainments are "hard to swallow." Rochester police confirmed they were notified of ICE operations but did not assist.

## Witness Accounts
Community organizer Ryan Perez (COPAL Minnesota): "We're gathering to make sure that ICE knows they're not welcome here and Rochester's a place of love and support."

David Perdomo (COPAL MN): "There is a feeling of fear in our community — fear of being detained or someone that they love being detained and left alone."

Pablo Sgaki, protester: "I myself, as an immigrant before, knows what it's like to live in fear sometimes."
