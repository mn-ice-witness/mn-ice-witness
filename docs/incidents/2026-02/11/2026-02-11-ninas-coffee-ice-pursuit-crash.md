# Multi-Car Crash Outside Nina's Coffee After Federal Agent Pursuit in St. Paul

## Summary
A man in a red Toyota Prius crashed into two vehicles at the intersection of Selby and Western avenues in St. Paul around 9:30 a.m. on February 11 after being pursued by federal agents. The man and a woman in one of the struck vehicles were both hospitalized with non-life-threatening injuries. Three cars were damaged. The man escaped through his car window and fled on foot before being caught by agents. Competing accounts emerged immediately: a federal agent at the scene told bystanders the man had committed a hit-and-run a mile back, while social media accounts described an ICE agent causing the crash and leaving the scene. A large crowd gathered, yelling at the dozen-plus federal agents who responded.

## Competing Narratives

### Federal Account
A federal agent at the scene told protesters and bystanders that the man in the red Prius had been involved in a hit-and-run approximately one mile from the crash site, and agents were pursuing him.

### Social Media / Witness Account
Aaron Rupar reported on Threads: "Very bad car accident involving ice agent who left the scene." Multiple social media accounts described the incident as an ICE agent causing the crash and fleeing. The framing circulated on Threads, Bluesky, and ResetEra forums.

### What Is Known
St. Paul Police confirmed that "federal agents were pursuing a person in a vehicle when the vehicle crashed." The Prius spun out of control at the intersection, struck two cars, and stopped when it hit a chunk of ice. Three vehicles were damaged. The man escaped through his car window and fled on foot before being caught by agents.
