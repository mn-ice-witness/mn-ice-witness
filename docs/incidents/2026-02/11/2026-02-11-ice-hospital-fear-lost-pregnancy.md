# ICE Fears Cause Lost Pregnancy, Home Birth Crisis as Clinic No-Show Rates Hit 60%

## Summary
Multiple Tier 1 news outlets reported that ICE presence in Minnesota hospitals and clinics has caused measurable medical harm. A pregnant woman lost her pregnancy at 20 weeks after postponing a monitoring appointment due to fear of ICE. Another pregnant woman in her third trimester stopped attending visits entirely; a nurse found her at home 8 centimeters dilated, too terrified to seek help. Clinics across the metro report 50-60% no-show rates among immigrant patients. Health care facilities have begun setting up "safe rooms" for patients afraid of ICE encounters.
