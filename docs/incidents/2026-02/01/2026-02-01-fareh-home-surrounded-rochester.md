# Citizen Check: ICE Agents Surround U.S. Citizen Family Home in Rochester

## Summary
ICE agents surrounded the home of the Fareh family in Rochester on Sunday, February 1, 2026, while Abdirashid Fareh's siblings were home alone, including a 12-year-old sister with special needs. The family are U.S. citizens born in Rochester. No arrests were made, but the 12-year-old refused to go to school the following day. Fareh testified about the incident at the Rochester City Council meeting on February 2.

## Incident Details
Abdirashid Fareh testified at the Rochester City Council meeting on Monday, February 2, 2026, that ICE agents surrounded his family's home on Sunday, February 1, while his siblings were home alone.

Fareh told the council: "We are U.S. citizens. My siblings were born here in Rochester, but still this happened."

He noted his 12-year-old sister with special needs did not want to go to school on Monday due to the encounter.

## Context
The incident occurred during an extended period of ICE activity in Rochester. Rochester Police Chief James Franklin reported that local officers responded to 18 calls relating to federal agents within a seven- to nine-day period. At the same council meeting, the council voted 4-3 to direct the city attorney to coordinate efforts for submitting briefs supporting federal lawsuits related to ICE actions in Minnesota. The council unanimously reaffirmed Rochester as a "city of compassion."
