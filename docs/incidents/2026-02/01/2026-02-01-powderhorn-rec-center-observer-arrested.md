# Observer Arrested, Concussion Grenades Deployed at Powderhorn Rec Center

## Summary

On Sunday morning, February 1, 2026, federal agents staged at the Powderhorn Park Recreation Center parking lot. According to Council Member Jason Chavez, who was on site with Park Board Commissioner Carvajal Moran, agents "began to throw chemical irritants at observers" and detained one observer. An eyewitness who arrived at the scene reported hearing concussion grenades and finding a neighbor "on their knees asking for medical help" with a bleeding mouth.

### Council Member Jason Chavez

> "This morning, federal agents staged at the Powderhorn Park Recreation Center lot and began to throw chemical irritants at observers. They also kidnapped an observer. ## Eyewitness Account

From a Facebook post by an observer who arrived at the scene:

> "As I woke up, early morning alerts came in that a single car with three ICE agents in tactical gear was stalking the Powderhorn Park Rec. Center. Let's be clear why they might be doing that at 8:30 am - it's because they were looking to snatch parents from their young children as they made their way inside. That's who goes there at such an early hour on a weekend morning. They were looking to separate parents from their children.
>
> One neighbor / observer was already present. Then an alert said two more were there documenting the scene. Soon it became clear that more neighbors were needed. Minutes later I arrived to the sound of concussion grenades and a neighbor, mouth bleeding, on their knees asking for medical help, which fell on deaf ears as one agent stalked the crowd aiming his 'less lethal' weapon point blank at me and others.
>
> More cars filled with agents arrived. With our neighbor unlawfully abducted, needing medical attention, the three cars drove off, one agent casually pepper spraying neighbors in their faces through his passenger window as they left."

Another eyewitness wrote:

> "This is what I woke up to this morning. In the background my neighbor, on the ground, woke up to much worse for the 'crime' of observing a Federal agent breaking local and Federal law. Minneapolis is living under a violent, unlawful, and chaotic occupation."

## Context

The Powderhorn Park Recreation Center has been a site of previous ICE staging activity. The Minneapolis Park Board passed anti-ICE resolutions after federal agents were photographed using recreation centers to stage raids.
