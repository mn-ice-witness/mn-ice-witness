# Chief Judge Schiltz Documents 210 Court Order Violations, Threatens Criminal Contempt

## Summary
Chief U.S. District Judge Patrick Schiltz issued a 41-page ruling on Feb. 27 documenting 210 violations of court orders across 143 immigration cases during Operation Metro Surge — 113 new violations on top of 97 previously identified. Schiltz, a George W. Bush appointee, threatened criminal contempt including imprisonment against U.S. Attorney Daniel Rosen and ICE officials, writing: "The Court is not aware of another occasion in the history of the United States in which a federal court has had to threaten contempt — again and again and again — to force the United States government to comply with court orders." Violations included missed release deadlines, failures to file status updates, and unauthorized transfer of a detainee to Texas despite a court order. The U.S. Attorney's office had dropped from 64 prosecutors to 36 while handling over 1,000 habeas petitions.
