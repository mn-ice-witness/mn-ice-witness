# Shakopee Student Stopped by Immigration Agents Near School

## Summary
Emily Rojas, a Shakopee High School student, was a passenger in a car stopped by immigration agents while leaving school on Feb. 16 — four days after Border Czar Tom Homan announced the end of Operation Metro Surge, and one week before DHS told Congress that agents had been directed not to stage near schools. Multiple ICE vehicles were present. Rojas said she was not afraid for herself but feared for another person in the car. She described the encounter at a Unidos MN rally at the State Capitol on Feb. 26.
