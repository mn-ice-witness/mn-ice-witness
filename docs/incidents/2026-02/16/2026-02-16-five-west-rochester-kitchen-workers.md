# Two Kitchen Workers Detained at Five West Restaurant in Rochester

## Summary
On February 16, 2026 — four days after border czar Tom Homan announced the end of Operation Metro Surge — six ICE agents entered through the back door of Five West Kitchen & Bar in Rochester before the restaurant opened and detained two kitchen employees. DHS identified the men as David Hernandez-Santos and Ivan Fosado Ramirez-Gregorio, both from Mexico. Ramirez-Gregorio was charged with felony illegal re-entry after prior removal. The restaurant and its sister location Smoak BBQ closed for the day. Rep. Andy Smith (DFL-Rochester) confirmed the arrests.

## Timeline
