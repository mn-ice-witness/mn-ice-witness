# Minneapolis Estimates $203 Million Economic Impact from Operation Metro Surge

## Summary
City of Minneapolis officials released a preliminary impact assessment showing Operation Metro Surge caused an estimated $203.1 million in documented economic damage to the city. The largest category was $81 million in business and restaurant losses, followed by $47 million in lost worker wages from people afraid to leave their homes. Emergency Management Director Rachel Sayre called the figures "almost certainly undercounts."

## Economic Impact Breakdown
- **Total documented impact:** $203.1 million
- **Business and restaurant losses:** $81 million
- **Lost worker wages:** $47 million (workers afraid to leave homes)
- **Hotel revenue losses:** $4.7 million (cancellations extending through summer)

## Human Impact
- 76,200 people experienced food insecurity
- 8,713 school-aged children in need of mental health services
- 50% reduction in mental health service client contact

## Key Quotes

### Rachel Sayre (City Emergency Management Director)
"This preliminary assessment is by no means exhaustive, and these totals are almost certainly undercounts."

### Mayor Jacob Frey
Called on federal and state governments to provide financial assistance to Minneapolis. Urged people to "support these immigrant-owned businesses that have added so greatly to the fabric of our communities."
