# Dozen Agents Shatter Semi Truck Window at Flying J Travel Center; At Least Two Arrested

## Summary
Approximately a dozen ICE agents in plain clothes and tactical vests surrounded a semi truck at the Flying J Pilot Travel Center between Lonsdale and Northfield. When occupants refused to open the window, an agent climbed the side step and shattered the glass, then reached through to unlock the door. At least two people were pulled from the truck and arrested. Two agents fist-bumped after the arrests. A Rice County Sheriff's deputy and Sergeant Ross Spicer briefly confronted the ICE officers.

## Official Accounts

### Truck Owner
Ben Goren (Skyway Movers Limited, Wood Dale, IL): "I know, they just told me. I have to find someone to retrieve the truck now, and I guess [I have to pay for it]." Goren said he was unaware of any criminal history and noted the driver is a blood relative.

### Local Law Enforcement
Rice County Sheriff's deputies responded and briefly confronted ICE officers. Sgt. Ross Spicer spoke with agents at the scene.

## Witness Accounts
Ten photographs documented the full arrest sequence. The Flying J has been a recurring staging area for ICE operations; management reported multiple arrests at the location over the past year, with vehicles frequently left behind.

MN Rep. Kristi Pursell witnessed and photographed agents conducting a separate operation outside Northfield City Hall on the same morning. Northfield Supporting Neighbors' ICE alert line received multiple reports of high federal activity throughout Northfield on Tuesday.
