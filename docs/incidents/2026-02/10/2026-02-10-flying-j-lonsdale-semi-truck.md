# Dozen Agents Shatter Semi Truck Window at Flying J Travel Center; At Least Two Arrested

## Summary
Approximately a dozen ICE agents in plain clothes and tactical vests surrounded a semi truck at the Flying J Pilot Travel Center between Lonsdale and Northfield. When occupants refused to open the window, an agent climbed the side step and shattered the glass with a small object, then pushed through to unlock the door. At least two people were pulled from the truck and arrested. Two agents fist-bumped after the arrests. A Rice County Sheriff's deputy and Sergeant Ross Spicer briefly confronted the ICE officers.

## Official Accounts

### Truck Owner
Ben Goren (Skyway Movers Limited, Wood Dale, IL): "I know, they just told me. I have to find someone to retrieve the truck now, and I guess [I have to pay for it]." Goren: "I don't know of any criminal history." He noted that he and the driver are blood-related.

### Local Law Enforcement
A deputy and Sgt. Ross Spicer of the Rice County Sheriff's Office confronted ICE officers after the arrest, having a brief discussion with the maskless agent.

## Witness Accounts
Ten photographs documented the full arrest sequence. Volunteer Gina Washburn of Northfield Supporting Neighbors reported that an ICE agent previously confirmed the Flying J serves as a staging area for operations. Management has witnessed numerous arrests over the past year, with vehicles frequently left behind.

MN Rep. Kristi Pursell witnessed and photographed agents conducting a separate operation outside Northfield City Hall on the same morning. Northfield Supporting Neighbors' ICE alert line received multiple reports of high federal activity throughout Northfield on Tuesday.
