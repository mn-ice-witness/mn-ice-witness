# ICE Agents Fake Car Trouble to Lure Columbia Heights Man Outside; Arrested and Sent to Texas

## Summary
ICE agents staged a fake car breakdown in front of Jesus Flores' Columbia Heights home to lure him outside. Security camera footage shows Flores leaving to help two people examining a vehicle's hood, then two additional cars arriving to block the street on both sides as agents swarmed his driveway. Flores was transported to El Paso, Texas within 48 hours, which his family says was likely in violation of a federal judge's order.

Wife Dionicia Flores described her husband as a lifeline for their six children.
