# Asylum-Seeking Mother Arrested at Routine Check-In with Two Children; Released with Ankle Monitor

## Summary
A Mexican asylum-seeking mother identified by the pseudonym "Yolanda" went to a routine ISAP (Intensive Supervision Appearance Program) in-person check-in at the Whipple Building. She was arrested along with her two sons and detained for seven hours inside the building. After ICE discovered she had a pending asylum application, she was released but fitted with an ankle monitor with no explanation. Officers claimed she had failed to follow check-in instructions, though her family said this was false — she had been complying for years, first by answering phone calls for six years, then by submitting photos via an app for three years.
