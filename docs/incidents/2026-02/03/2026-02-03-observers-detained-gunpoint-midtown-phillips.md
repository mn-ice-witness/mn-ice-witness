# Observers Detained at Gunpoint in Midtown Phillips

## Summary
At least two observers were detained at gunpoint by ICE agents in the Midtown Phillips neighborhood of south Minneapolis. When confronted about drawing weapons, an agent claimed the observers had been making threatening hand symbols—specifically "finger guns"—not actual firearms.

### ICE Agent Statement
An agent on scene claimed the observers had been threatening them with "hand guns." When asked to clarify, the agent stated they meant the observers were making finger gun gestures, not brandishing actual weapons.
