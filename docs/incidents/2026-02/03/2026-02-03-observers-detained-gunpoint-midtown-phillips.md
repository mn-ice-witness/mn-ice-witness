# Observers Detained at Gunpoint, Bystanders Tackled in Midtown Phillips

## Summary
In two separate confrontations four blocks apart in the Midtown Phillips neighborhood, ICE agents drew handguns on observers for making "finger gun" gestures and later tackled at least two bystanders after two protesters smashed windows on two federal vehicles. The agents who tackled bystanders did not catch the people who broke the windows.

- **Citizenship:** Unknown
- **Role:** Observers tracking ICE activity; bystanders near federal vehicles

## Timeline
- **Morning** — ICE agents draw handguns and detain at least two observers at 18th Ave S and 27th St E. Agent claims observers were making threatening "finger gun" hand symbols.
- **~11:00 a.m.** — Four blocks south at 14th Ave S and 27th St E, two federal SUVs are parked on either side of the road. Approximately 30 protesters gather. Agents remain in vehicles.
- **~11:30 a.m.** — After a roughly 30-minute standoff, two men smash windows on two federal vehicles. Agents leap out and chase protesters. At least two bystanders are tackled and detained — neither was the person who broke the windows. Agents brandish pepper spray and tasers but do not deploy them. Additional federal vehicles and over a dozen agents arrive before the group drives away.

## Official Accounts

### ICE Agent Statement
An agent on scene claimed the observers had been threatening them with "hand guns." When asked to clarify, the agent stated they meant the observers were making finger gun gestures, not brandishing actual weapons.
