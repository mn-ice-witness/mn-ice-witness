# Fridley Superintendent Says ICE Followed Her to Work; Board Members Report ICE Outside Homes

## Updates
- **Feb 3** - [Star Tribune reports](https://www.startribune.com/walz-demands-dhs-stop-detaining-minnesota-children/601575923) Lewis appeared at Gov. Walz press conference saying ICE retaliation is "escalating every single day." ICE agents staged at two Fridley schools on January 31.

## Summary

Fridley Public Schools Superintendent Brenda Lewis reported on January 29, 2026, that ICE agents followed her to work in the morning and again in the evening while attending a bingo night at one of the district's schools. Three school board members also reported ICE agents outside their homes that day, with one board member also being followed.

## What Happened

According to Superintendent Lewis's LinkedIn post:

- Lewis was followed by ICE on her way to work on the morning of January 29.
- She was followed again in the evening while attending a bingo night at one of the district's schools.
- Three school board members reported ICE agents outside their homes the same day.
- One board member was also followed by ICE.

**From her LinkedIn post:** "I am once again asking elected officials to put a stop to this. This is about safety. This is about learning. Our children do not deserve to have their school experiences interrupted by fear. ... And no one — no educator, no public servant, no neighbor — deserves to live under this kind of pressure."

## Context: Fridley Schools and ICE

Fridley Public Schools serves about 2,700 students north of Minneapolis with significant Hispanic and Somali populations. The district:

- Closed schools on January 9, 2026, due to ICE activity concerns.
- Has 80% students of color.
- Has 16% of students enrolled in virtual learning due to fear.
- Passed a board resolution on December 16 refusing to grant ICE access without valid judicial warrants.

Lewis previously stated: "We're at a place where, in my 26 years, I have never seen a federal enforcement agency cause such deep, intentional chaos."
