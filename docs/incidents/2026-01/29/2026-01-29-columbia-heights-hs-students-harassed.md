# Columbia Heights High School Students Harassed by ICE Agents

## Summary
Three Columbia Heights High School students had their cars surrounded by ICE vehicles while lawfully driving to school on the morning of January 29. One 16-year-old student was forced to show their passport to masked federal agents. Another student called the school principal in fear, leaving a voicemail saying agents were "looming around the high school." Superintendent Zena Stenvik reported ICE agents were parked behind the high school in a park and circling schools that morning.
