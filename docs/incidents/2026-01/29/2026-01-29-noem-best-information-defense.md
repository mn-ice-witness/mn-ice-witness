# Noem Defends "Domestic Terrorist" Claim: "Best Information We Had at the Time"

## Summary

On January 29, 2026, DHS Secretary Kristi Noem defended her characterization of Alex Pretti as a "domestic terrorist" by stating officials were using "the best information we had at the time." She described the scene as "chaotic" but did not retract the claim. This statement came after her own agency's internal report to Congress made no mention of Pretti attacking officers or brandishing a gun—directly contradicting her initial public statements.

## The Statement

When asked about labeling Pretti a "domestic terrorist," Secretary Noem stated:

> "We were using the best information we had at the time."

She described the scene as "chaotic" but notably did not retract the characterization.

## Contradiction: DHS Internal Report

The DHS internal report submitted to Congress made **no mention** of:
- Pretti attacking officers
- Pretti brandishing a gun
- A "violent riot" as initially claimed

The report detailed:
- Agents pepper-sprayed Pretti and a woman before trying to take him into custody
- An agent yelled "He's got a gun!" multiple times
- Two immigration officers fired during the encounter

This directly contradicts the initial public statements from Noem and other officials who claimed Pretti posed an imminent threat.
