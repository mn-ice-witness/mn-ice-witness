# Second St. Paul Public Works Employee with Legal Work Status Detained

## Summary
A second St. Paul Public Works employee was detained by ICE during the week of January 29, 2026. Public Works Director Sean Kershaw stated the worker "had full legal right to work" and was detained "based purely on their country of origin." The employee is now being held in El Paso, Texas and is connected with a lawyer.
