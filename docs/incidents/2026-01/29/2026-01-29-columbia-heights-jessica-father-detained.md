# Woman Grabbed While Warming Car; Father Detained as "Original Target"

## Summary

On the morning of January 29, 2026, ICE agents detained a man in Columbia Heights while his daughter Jessica was warming up her car. An agent grabbed Jessica "very forcefully" and held her wrist and arm for at least five minutes before releasing her when they confirmed she was not their target. Her father, an undocumented immigrant from Ecuador with no criminal record, was taken into custody. Jessica's husband had been detained approximately three weeks earlier, leaving her as the sole caregiver for her daughters.

## What Happened

Early Thursday morning, Jessica went outside to warm up her car on a residential street in Columbia Heights. An ICE agent grabbed her "very forcefully" and detained her, holding her wrist and arm for at least five minutes.

**Jessica:** "I told them, 'I haven't done anything. Why are you taking me?'"

ICE ultimately released Jessica after determining she was not named on their warrant. They detained her father, who she described as their "original target."

Jessica brought her family from Ecuador about five years ago, fleeing gang violence. Her father has no criminal record.

A neighbor recorded the incident on video.

### Impact on Family

With her husband detained three weeks earlier and now her father taken, Jessica is the sole caregiver for her daughters.
