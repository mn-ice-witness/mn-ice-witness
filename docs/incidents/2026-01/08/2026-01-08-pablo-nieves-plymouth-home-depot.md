# Daycare Handyman with Pending Asylum Case Detained at Home Depot, Sent to Texas for Two Weeks

## Updates
- **Feb 18** - [Star Tribune profile](https://www.startribune.com/ice-crackdown-in-minnesota-hits-asylum-seekers/601571134) reveals Nieves is a Venezuelan asylum-seeker who overstayed a nonimmigrant visa, not a legal resident as previously reported. He was released on bond Jan. 24 and returned to Minnesota. DHS 30-day appeal window closed Feb. 17. Entry reclassified from citizens to immigrants and corrected.

## Summary
Pablo Nieves, a handyman at the Mis Amigos Spanish Immersion preschool chain, was detained by ICE on January 8 in a Home Depot parking lot in Plymouth. Nieves, a Venezuelan asylum-seeker who arrived in 2018, had a pending case with no criminal record and held work authorization.

He was transferred to a detention center in El Paso, Texas, where he was held for two weeks. On January 16, a judge ordered his release on bond. He returned to Minnesota on January 24. The DHS 30-day appeal window closed February 17; the family's asylum case continues.
