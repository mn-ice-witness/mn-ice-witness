# Work Permit Holder Detained at Bus Stop; Agents Knee on Neck, Pepper Sprayed

## Summary
"Freddy" (pseudonym), a work permit holder with pending U-visa and asylum applications who arrived from El Salvador in 2020, was detained by ICE while waiting at a Brooklyn Center bus stop after finishing his shift as a landscaper on January 8. Agents exited an unmarked car without identifying themselves or presenting a warrant. He ran to a nearby intersection hoping observers would record, but agents pinned him down, put a knee on his neck, and pepper-sprayed him in the face. He was taken to the Whipple Federal Building at Fort Snelling and held for several days before being transferred to Freeborn County Jail, where he was held for nearly a month. He was released on Feb. 8 without any of his documents.
