# DHS Response: "Radical Rhetoric" From "Sanctuary Politicians" Causes 1,300% Increase in Assaults

## Summary
On January 8, 2026, DHS issued a press release claiming a 1,300% increase in assaults on ICE officers and a 3,200% increase in vehicular attacks, blaming "sanctuary politicians" and their "radical rhetoric" for inciting violence against law enforcement. The release specifically cited Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey's statements. DHS has refused to provide underlying data for these claims; independent analysis by NPR and CPR News found the actual increase in federal court filings for assault on federal officers was approximately 25%.

4. CPR News (Oct 2, 2025): [Claims of huge rise in assaults against ICE drive responses, but aren't seen in available data](https://www.cpr.org/2025/10/02/ice-agent-assault-claims-data-lacking/)

## The Statement

DHS Assistant Secretary Tricia McLaughlin stated:

> "This unprecedented increase in violence against law enforcement is a direct result of sanctuary politicians and the media creating an environment that demonizes our law enforcement and encourages rampant assaults against them. Dangerous criminals — whether they be illegal aliens or U.S. citizens — are assaulting law enforcement and turning their vehicles into weapons to attack law enforcement."

> "Still, the brave men and women of DHS will not be deterred and will continue arresting the worst of the worst criminal illegal aliens. Anyone who attacks law enforcement will be prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law."

### Claims Made
- 275 assaults against ICE officers from Jan 20 - Dec 31, 2025, compared to 19 during the same period in 2024 (1,300% increase)
- 66 vehicular attacks since Jan 21, 2025, compared to 2 during the same period the previous year (3,200% increase)
- 8,000% increase in death threats against ICE officers

## Fact-Check

DHS has refused to provide underlying data for these statistics despite repeated requests from NPR and other outlets. Independent analysis found:

- **NPR (Oct 2025):** Federal court records showed only approximately a 25% rise in charges for assault against federal officers, not 1,000%+.
- **CPR News (Oct 2025):** DHS claimed progressively larger percentages over months (413% to 500% to 700% to 830% to 1,000%+ to 1,300%) without explanation. Small baselines produce dramatic percentages — going from 2 vehicular attacks to 66 is 3,200%, but the absolute number is small.
- Grand juries rejected many assault charges: in Los Angeles, prosecutors sought indictments against approximately 38 protest-related defendants; grand juries approved only 7.

## Minnesota Connection
The release was issued the day after the shooting of [Renee Good](#2026-01-07-renee-good-shooting). DHS specifically cited Mayor Frey's statement telling ICE to "get the f--- out of Minneapolis" as an example of inflammatory rhetoric.
