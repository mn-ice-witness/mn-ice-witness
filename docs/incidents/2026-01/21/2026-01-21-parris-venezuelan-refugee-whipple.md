# Venezuelan Refugee Detained at Whipple Check-In, Flown to Texas with 60 Minnesota Women; Green Card Approved While in Custody

## Summary
A Venezuelan woman who entered the United States legally as a refugee in September 2024 received a letter ordering her to appear at the Whipple Federal Building on January 21. Instead of a routine check-in, she was detained, shackled at the ankles, not fed for 24 hours, and within hours loaded onto a plane to El Paso with busloads of other detainees. She was held with approximately 60 other Minnesota women. Her green card application was approved while she was in detention. She was released after Judge Tunheim issued a Temporary Restraining Order halting Operation PARRIS arrests on January 28.

- **Within hours** - Processed and loaded onto a plane to El Paso, Texas, with multiple buses of detainees.

- **Jan 27** - Called in for interview with USCIS officer and attorney about her time in Venezuela and reasons for leaving.
- **Jan 28** - Judge John Tunheim issues Temporary Restraining Order halting Operation PARRIS arrests and ordering detainees transported back to Minnesota within five days.
- **Early Feb** - Released. Received a single document showing her green card application had been approved. All other documents — Minnesota ID, passport, refugee approval notice — retained by ICE.
