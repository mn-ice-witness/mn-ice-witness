# Venezuelan Refugee Detained at Whipple Check-In, Flown to Texas with 60 Minnesota Women; Green Card Approved While in Custody

## Summary
A Venezuelan woman who entered the United States legally as a refugee was detained at a routine Whipple Federal Building check-in on January 21 and flown to El Paso, where she was held with approximately 60 other Minnesota women. Her green card application was approved while in detention, and she was released after Judge Tunheim's Temporary Restraining Order halting Operation PARRIS arrests on January 28.
