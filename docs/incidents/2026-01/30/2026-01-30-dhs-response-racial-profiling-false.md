# DHS Response: Racial Profiling Allegations Are "Disgusting, Reckless and Categorically FALSE"

## Summary

On January 30, 2026, DHS Assistant Secretary for Public Affairs Tricia McLaughlin issued a statement categorically denying that ICE engages in racial profiling. The statement came as multiple U.S. citizens reported being stopped, detained, and in some cases injured by federal agents based on their appearance rather than any immigration violation.

## Sources

1. NPR (Feb 1, 2026): [Minnesota citizens detained by ICE are left rattled, even weeks later](https://www.npr.org/2026/02/01/nx-s1-5689031/minnesota-citizens-detained-ice)
2. MPR News (Feb 1, 2026): [Minnesota citizens detained by ICE are left rattled](https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/02/01/npr-minnesota-citizens-detained-ice)

## The Statement

DHS Assistant Secretary Tricia McLaughlin stated:

> "These allegations that ICE engages in 'racial profiling' are disgusting, reckless and categorically FALSE."

> "What makes someone a target for immigration enforcement is if they are illegally in the U.S.— NOT their skin color, race, or ethnicity."
