# Citing Safety Concerns, Ecuadorian Family Self-Deports Despite Pending Asylum Case

## Summary
Carlos Albuja, his wife Esther, and their daughters left their Minneapolis apartment at 2:45 a.m. on January 30, 2026, with one-way tickets to Ecuador. The family had pending asylum cases and no criminal record, but decided to leave after Albuja was blocked by federal agents near the scene of Renee Good's shooting while driving to work. KSTP's 5 INVESTIGATES traveled with the family and documented the departure.
