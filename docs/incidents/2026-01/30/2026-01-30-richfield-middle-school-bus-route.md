# Federal Agents Present on Richfield Middle School Bus Route

## Summary
Richfield Public Schools confirmed federal agents were present on a school bus route on January 30, 2026. District leadership traveled to the bus and staff walked students to their homes. Superintendent Steven Unowsky confirmed all students were safe.
