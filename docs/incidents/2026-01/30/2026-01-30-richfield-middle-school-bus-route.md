# Federal Agents Present on Richfield Middle School Bus Route

## Summary
Richfield Public Schools confirmed federal agents were present on a school bus route on January 30, 2026. Once school staff realized agents were on the route, district leadership traveled to the bus to ensure student safety. Staff and vetted volunteers walked students from the bus to their homes. Superintendent Steven Unowsky confirmed all students were safe and urged students to reach out to school counselors if feeling anxious.

## Context
This incident occurred as schools across the Twin Cities responded to fears about ICE presence near schools. Minneapolis and St. Paul Public Schools had already offered e-learning options for January due to concerns about ICE agents taking students. Richfield and District 196 followed suit with virtual learning options.

Multiple minors have been taken from Minnesota schools during Operation Metro Surge, including 5-year-old Liam Ramos from Columbia Heights, who is now detained in Texas.
