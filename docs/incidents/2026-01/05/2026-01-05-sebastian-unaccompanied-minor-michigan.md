# Ecuadorian Teen Labeled "Unaccompanied Minor" by ICE, Sent to Michigan Shelter; Family Searched for Him for a Week

## Summary
A 16-year-old Ecuadorian asylum-seeker was detained by ICE in north Minneapolis in early January, labeled an "unaccompanied minor" despite having a father in the U.S., and transferred to a youth shelter in Michigan. His family and attorney lost track of his whereabouts for nearly a week before his attorney traced him and secured his release on January 26.
