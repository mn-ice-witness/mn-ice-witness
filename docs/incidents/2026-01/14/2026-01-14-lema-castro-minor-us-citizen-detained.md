# Minor U.S. Citizen and Lema-Castro Siblings Detained After ICE Follows Car

## Summary
ICE agents followed a car in Minnesota and arrested all three occupants: Diana Lema-Castro (20, Ecuadorian national), her brother William Lema-Castro (19, Ecuadorian national), and their friend, a minor U.S. citizen identified as "Jane Doe" in court filings. Both Lema-Castro siblings had pending Special Immigrant Juvenile Status applications. A joint habeas corpus petition was filed January 14, 2026 (Doe v. Bondi, 0:26-cv-00293); the court enjoined removal from Minnesota and severed the cases. Despite the injunction, Diana was transferred to Texas on January 15. Judge Nancy Brasel granted both siblings' habeas petitions on January 18, ordering immediate return to Minnesota and release. The minor's case was voluntarily dismissed on January 20.
