## Summary
On January 14, 2026, an ICE agent shot Julio Cesar Sosa-Celis, 24, in the leg during an arrest operation in north Minneapolis that began with a case of mistaken identity. DHS claims Sosa-Celis and two others attacked the agent with shovels. His partner, Indriany Mendoza Camacho, who was present and witnessed the events, disputes this — she says Sosa-Celis never grabbed anything and only tried to get inside the house. According to the defense, the agent fired through the closed front door after the men had gone inside. The bullet grazed Sosa-Celis' right thigh, then traveled through a wall into a first-floor apartment where four children and an adult were present, landing near a child's playpen. Both Sosa-Celis and co-defendant Alfredo Aljorna had Temporary Protected Status and were seeking U Visas. Agents later shot a tear gas canister through the apartment window. The two women present were detained, shackled at the feet, hands, and waist, and flown to a Texas detention center.

## Timeline

- **Pursuit** - Sosa-Celis flees in his vehicle, crashes into a parked car, then flees on foot to the duplex he shares with Aljorna and the two women.
- **Confrontation outside** - ICE agent and Sosa-Celis struggle outside the duplex. DHS claims Aljorna and another man attacked the agent with a snow shovel and broom. Partner Mendoza Camacho, who witnessed the events, says Sosa-Celis "never grabbed anything" and "only tried to separate them so they could both get into the house."
- **Inside the duplex** - Aljorna breaks free. Both men enter the home and close the door.

- **Barricade** - The four adults and two children rush upstairs, place a dresser behind the door. Mendoza Camacho calls her mother on video: "They're going to kill us, Mom."

## Official Accounts

### DHS Statement (Jan 15)
DHS described a "targeted traffic stop" and claimed Sosa-Celis "fled in his vehicle, crashed into a parked car, then fled on foot." DHS stated that during the arrest, "two subjects came out of a nearby apartment and attacked the law enforcement officer with a snow shovel and broom handle" and that Sosa-Celis "began striking the officer with a shovel or broomstick." The agent fired "a defensive shot to defend his life."

DHS cited Sosa-Celis' criminal background: convicted of driving without a license and arrested for two counts of giving a false name to a peace officer. CBS Minnesota noted DHS "did not share any information indicating he was wanted for a violent crime."

### FBI Affidavit (Jan 22)
The FBI affidavit revealed ICE originally targeted someone else entirely. The shooting resulted from a case of mistaken identity. The two men pursued were longtime friends and fellow DoorDash drivers, not the original target.

### Defense Account
Both Aljorna and Sosa-Celis maintain the gunshot came after they had gone inside the duplex and closed the front door. This is corroborated by the bullet hole in the front door and the bullet's trajectory through interior walls.

## Context: ICE Interference with State Criminal Cases

A court filing in the Hussen v. Noem case includes a statement from John Choi, Ramsey County Attorney, documenting that ICE has removed defendants of criminal cases — involving criminal sexual conduct and child solicitation — before the perpetrators could be convicted.

> "In one case, RCAO received information that a crime victim for an ongoing case who is understood to have legal status and documentation was picked up by DHS and transported to Texas immediately; it is unclear whether that crime victim will be available to provide their testimony."

The accelerated deportation of Valentina Moreno — a witness whose testimony contradicts the government's account of this shooting — follows the same pattern of removing individuals critical to legal proceedings.

## Endangerment of Children
The bullet fired by the ICE agent traveled through the closed front door, through Sosa-Celis' leg, through another wall, and into a first-floor apartment where it lodged in the wall near a child's playpen used as a crib. Four children and one adult were present in the room at the time. A tear gas canister was also fired through an apartment window.
