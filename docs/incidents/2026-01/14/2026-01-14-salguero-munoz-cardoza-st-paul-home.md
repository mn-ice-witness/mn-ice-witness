# Family Divided After ICE Enters St. Paul Home With Non-Judicial Warrant, Daughter Deported

## Summary
ICE agents entered a St. Paul family's home on January 14 with a warrant of removal for a family member who no longer lived at the address. Multiple attorneys stated the document was not a judicial warrant signed by a judge and did not authorize lawful entry. Agents detained Alfredo Salguero, a TPS holder for over 20 years, and his daughter Astrid Munoz Cardoza, who is undocumented. Salguero was held at Kandiyohi County Jail for 21 days before being released on bond February 2. Munoz Cardoza was transferred to detention in Texas and deported to El Salvador on February 8 after signing voluntary departure papers when denied the opportunity to consult her family. Neither had a criminal record beyond misdemeanor driving offenses. ICE said the operation targeted a fugitive sex offender who was not found at the residence.
