# ICE Conducts Door-to-Door Operations in Pelican Rapids

## Summary
ICE agents conducted door-to-door operations throughout Pelican Rapids, arresting at least one person: a 43-year-old Nicaraguan man who had lived in the community for three years. The operation caused significant community disruption, with 30 students staying home from school the next day and a local Catholic church canceling its Hispanic Mass. A peaceful protest drew 75-100 participants the following day.

## What Happened
- ICE agents knocked on doors throughout the community.
- At least one person confirmed arrested: a 43-year-old Nicaraguan man who had lived in Pelican Rapids for three years.
- Five unmarked vehicles were reported in town.
- Agents had a list of names and addresses.
- Pelican Rapids Chief of Police Todd Quaintance confirmed ICE presence.

## Community Impact

- 30 students did not attend school the following Monday.
