# Soccer Academy Player's Mother Detained at Minneapolis Hotel During Tournament

## Summary
The mother of a Forward Madison FC Academy player was detained by immigration authorities at their hotel in Minneapolis while the team was in the Twin Cities for weekend league matches. After the U19 match, the player and his mother went out for lunch and found immigration authorities at their hotel when they returned. The mother was arrested and transferred to custody in Texas, leaving the 18-year-old high school senior from Milwaukee to fend for himself. The exact date of detention is approximate (weekend of Jan 10-11, 2026).
