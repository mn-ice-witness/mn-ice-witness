# Green Card Holder Roughed Up by Agents, Held Over 10 Hours, Offered Money to Identify Immigrants

## Summary
Dennis Hernandez Ramirez, a 32-year-old Green Card holder who has lived in the U.S. for 25 years, was pulled over and "roughed up" by at least four federal agents while driving in Minneapolis. He was held for more than 10 hours at the Whipple Federal Building, where agents offered him money and legal protection in exchange for helping identify immigrants.

## Affected Individual(s)
- **Name:** Dennis Hernandez Ramirez
- **Age:** 32
- **Citizenship:** Green Card holder (legal permanent resident)
- **Background:** Born in Mexico, lived in the U.S. for 25 years. Moved to Minneapolis from North Carolina approximately six weeks before the arrest for work.

## Treatment During Arrest
- Tight handcuffs cut his wrist.
- One agent held him down and "thoroughly dug his elbow into my chest" despite Hernandez Ramirez not moving.
- An agent "shoved their hand in their pocket, and then they pulled my head back and shoved their finger in my nose." He speculated agents were trying to use a chemical to subdue him.

## At Whipple Federal Building
- Made to wait in a semi-enclosed garage before being taken to a holding cell.
- Placed in shackles on his feet.
- Described cells as packed "shoulder to shoulder."
- Told he would be charged with assaulting officers.
- Feared deportation to Mexico.
- Agents offered him money and legal protection to help identify immigrants.

## Key Quotes

On the offer from agents:
> "Why would you guys threaten the hell out of me just to tell me, 'hey, it's gonna be fine if you help us?' It made me feel very unsettled and kind of sick."
