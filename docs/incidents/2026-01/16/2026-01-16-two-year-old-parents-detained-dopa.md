# Two-Year-Old Left Without Parents After ICE Detains Both; DOPA Form Prevents Foster Care

## Updates
- **Feb 13** — CBS Minnesota [reports the family has been partially reunited](https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/family-partially-reunited-ice-agent-arrests-minnesota/). The mother, identified as Janeth (Ecuadorian asylum seeker), and her children were released and returned to Minnesota, given one year to pursue an asylum claim. The father was deported to Ecuador. The family friend Jissica, who had legal authority through the DOPA form, cared for Scarlett during the separation.

## Summary
A two-year-old girl named Scarlett was left without either parent after ICE detained her father approximately three weeks before January 16, 2026, and her mother Janeth approximately four days before that date. The family — Janeth, her husband, their 4-year-old son, and 2-year-old Scarlett — were pulled over in a traffic stop in mid-January. The parents and 4-year-old were detained and transported to a Texas facility. Scarlett was left with a family friend named Jissica, who had legal authority through a previously signed Delegation of Parental Authority (DOPA) form. The father was deported to Ecuador. Janeth and the children were eventually released and reunited in Minnesota.

## Context: DOPA Forms in Minnesota

The Delegation of Parental Authority (DOPA) form allows parents to designate a trusted person to care for their children if they are detained. Immigrant advocacy organizations across Minnesota have been distributing DOPA forms and educating families about how to prepare for the possibility of detention during Operation Metro Surge.
