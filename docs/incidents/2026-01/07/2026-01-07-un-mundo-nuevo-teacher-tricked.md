# Daycare Teacher Tricked with Fake Car Accident Claim, Detained for 10 Minutes

## Summary
A teacher at Un Mundo Nuevo Children's Academy was detained by ICE on January 7, 2026, after being lured outside with a false claim that someone had hit her car. When she went outside to check, ICE agents detained her. The daycare told parents she is "a legal resident of this country." She was released approximately 10 minutes later.
