# U.S. Citizen Tackled by ICE at Shakopee Construction Site, Hospitalized

## Summary
Luis Ramirez Orozco, a Latino U.S. citizen, was tackled and detained by ICE agents at a construction site in Shakopee on the same morning Renee Good was shot by an ICE agent in Minneapolis. Agents surrounded him and smashed his head on the floor. He suffered bruised ribs and a concussion, requiring medical attention.
