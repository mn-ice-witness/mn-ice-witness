# Federal Agents Ram White Pickup Truck Into Protesters

## Summary
In the chaotic aftermath of the Renee Good shooting, federal agents drove a white pickup truck into a group of protesters as they turned into an alley off of 34th Street. Racket co-owner/editor Em Cassel witnessed and filmed the incident, posting video to Bluesky where it received significant engagement.
