# U.S. Citizen Detained at Olmsted County Government Center, Released After Identity Verified

## Summary
ICE agents followed a U.S. citizen of Latino heritage to the Olmsted County Government Center in Rochester on January 31, 2026, and detained him amid outcry from observers and local legislators. The man was released after his identity was verified. Rochester Police Chief Jim Franklin said DHS officials described the detainment as a traffic stop, which he said ICE is allowed to make if agents suspect a driver is undocumented.

## Incident Details
According to accounts at the February 2 Rochester City Council meeting:

1. ICE agents followed a U.S. citizen of Latino heritage to the Olmsted County Government Center on Saturday, January 31.
2. Agents detained the man at the government center.
3. Observers and local legislators witnessed the detention and expressed outcry.
4. The man was released after his identity was verified.

## Official Accounts

### Rochester Police Chief Jim Franklin
Franklin sought information from DHS officials on Monday (February 2). DHS told him the man was detained until his identity could be verified and then released. ICE agents described the incident as a "traffic stop," which Franklin said ICE is allowed to make if agents suspect a driver is undocumented.

### Representative Andy Smith
Smith shared video stating he personally verified the detained man's citizenship documents:
> "I have personally seen their passport, they were born here in this country, in MN."

### Rochester City Council (Feb 2)
Several council members and community speakers referenced this incident during a meeting where the council debated joining a lawsuit against the federal government over ICE enforcement. The council split on the issue.

## Context
This incident occurred during an extended period of ICE activity in Rochester. Rochester has been one of the most active areas for ICE enforcement in Minnesota, with 64 arrests reported in the city as of January 23.
