# Food Shelf Volunteer Detained at Basic Needs in Cottage Grove

## Summary

A middle-aged Latino pastor who volunteered at Basic Needs Food Market in Cottage Grove was detained by ICE agents on January 15, 2026. The man had been volunteering several times a week since October, providing translation services for Spanish-speaking customers. ICE agents surveilled the food pantry for two days before making the arrest in the parking lot.
