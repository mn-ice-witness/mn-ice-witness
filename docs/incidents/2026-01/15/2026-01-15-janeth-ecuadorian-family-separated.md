# Ecuadorian Family Separated in Traffic Stop; Father Deported, Mother and Children Eventually Reunited

## Summary
In mid-January, ICE agents pulled over a vehicle carrying Janeth (first name only), her husband, their 4-year-old son, and 2-year-old daughter Scarlett. The parents and 4-year-old were detained and transported to a Texas immigration facility. Scarlett was left with a family friend named Jissica, who had legal authority through a previously signed Delegation of Parental Authority form. The husband was deported to Ecuador. Janeth and her children were eventually released and reunited in Minnesota, given one year to pursue an asylum claim.
