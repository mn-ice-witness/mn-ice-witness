# Observer's Car Windows Smashed by ICE; Detained Three Hours at Whipple, Driver's License Never Returned

## Summary

Matthew Carlson, a 41-year-old child care worker, was detained by federal agents after following ICE vehicles — a common practice among people trying to document enforcement operations. When he refused to leave his car without an explanation, agents smashed his windows with batons, pulled him out, pushed him to the ground, and handcuffed him. He was held at the Whipple Federal Building for approximately three hours, during which at least 10 agents refused to explain why he was detained or allow him a lawyer. His driver's license was never returned. The exact date in January is unknown.
