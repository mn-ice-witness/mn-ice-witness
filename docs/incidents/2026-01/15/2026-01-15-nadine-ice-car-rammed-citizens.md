# ICE Rams Into Parked Car, Demands U.S. Citizen Couple Show Citizenship Papers

## Summary
In January 2026, ICE agents rammed into a parked car belonging to Minneapolis resident Nadine and her Latino U.S. citizen husband, then cornered them with three vehicles. Despite being citizens, agents demanded the husband provide proof of citizenship. When Nadine attempted to exit her vehicle to photograph the ICE vehicle's license plate for insurance purposes, agents threatened her with obstruction charges. Agents refused to provide names or badge numbers. Nadine later filed a police report and learned the ICE vehicle's plates could not be located in police systems.
