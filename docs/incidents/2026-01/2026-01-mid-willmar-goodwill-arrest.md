# Man and Wife Arrested by 12 ICE Agents in Willmar Goodwill Parking Lot; 15-Year-Old Threatened for Filming

## Summary
Twelve ICE agents in six vehicles arrested a man and his wife in the Goodwill parking lot in Willmar. Agents did not confirm the man's identity, did not allow him to show documentation, and did not allow him to speak with the Spanish-speaking ICE agent on scene. Fifteen-year-old Adyssey Barrett recorded the arrest on her phone and asked agents to produce a judicial warrant; agents threatened to arrest her. The wife was also handcuffed despite agents yelling confused questions about who she was.
