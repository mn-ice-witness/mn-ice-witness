# Legal Visa Holder Hospitalized With Skull Fractures; ICE Claims He "Purposely Ran Headfirst Into a Brick Wall"

## Updates
- **Jan 31** - [AP investigation](https://apnews.com/article/immigration-enforcement-minneapolis-hospitals-32cd5ca61d495e155d43b7cfd5e563b9) reveals HCMC medical staff believe his injuries are inconsistent with ICE's account. Alberto was discharged Jan 28; coworkers have taken him in. He has significant memory loss and faces a long recovery.
- **Jan 24** - Federal Judge Donovan Frank [orders immediate release](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/judge-orders-immediate-release-minnesota-014048216.html) from ICE custody. Unclear if ICE has complied.

## Summary
Alberto Castaneda Mondragon, who entered the U.S. legally on a temporary worker visa in 2022, was detained in St. Paul on January 8, 2026 during Operation Metro Surge. He sustained catastrophic head injuries including skull fractures and brain hemorrhages while in ICE custody. ICE claims he "purposely ran headfirst into a brick wall" while handcuffed. Hospital records show Alberto told staff he was "dragged and mistreated by federal agents." He has been hospitalized at Hennepin County Medical Center for nearly two weeks, is "only minimally responsive," and his condition is declining. Attorneys allege ICE has interfered with his medical care by shackling his legs to the hospital bed despite warnings from medical staff. A federal judge ordered his immediate release on January 24, 2026, noting he "was in the wrong place at the wrong time."

- Hemorrhages in at least 5 areas of the brain.

AP interviewed a doctor and five nurses at HCMC who said his injuries were inconsistent with running into a wall or an accidental fall.

## ICE's Explanation
ICE officers told hospital staff that Alberto was "laying down in handcuffs when he attempted to flee, and then, for unknown reasons, purposefully ran headfirst into a brick wall." One ICE statement described what happened as: he "got his s*** rocked."

After 15 days, ICE had still not completed intake processing.

## Alberto's Account
Hospital records show Alberto told HCMC staff he was "dragged and mistreated by federal agents."

## Attorney Allegations
- ICE has "interfered" with his medical care.
- Agents shackled his legs to the hospital bed despite warnings from medical staff.
- Family members were prevented from visiting.
- ICE has "largely refused to provide information" about what happened.

## Judge's Ruling (Jan 24, 2026)
District Judge Donovan Frank ordered immediate release from ICE custody, noting Alberto "was in the wrong place at the wrong time."

The lawsuit alleges: "There is no reason to believe that Mr. Castaneda Mondragon was arrested for any other reason than that he was a brown-skinned Latino Spanish-speaker at a location that immigration agents arbitrarily decided to target."
