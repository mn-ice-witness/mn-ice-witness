# Observer Pepper Sprayed in Face by Agent from Same Vehicle That Threatened Him Two Days Earlier

## Summary
Troy Carrillo, a 40-year-old Richfield resident, was pepper sprayed twice in the face by ICE agents while attempting to record them detaining an elderly woman in a Sam's Club parking lot in Bloomington on January 9, 2026. Carrillo recognized the vehicle as the same maroon Dodge Ram that had circled his car and pointed mace at him two days earlier at a nearby Target lot. The elderly woman agents had been detaining was later released because she had lawful status and had been unlawfully detained.

## Timeline

### January 7, 2026 — Target Parking Lot, American Boulevard and Penn Avenue, Bloomington
- Carrillo and his wife learn from a community group chat about ICE activity at the Target in Bloomington. They go to observe and support neighbors.
- They find four suspected ICE vehicles in the parking lot: a maroon Dodge Ram (Iowa plates), a white Chevy Impala, a Dodge Durango (Florida plates), and a white Jeep. Men with masks and vests visible through windshields. No other observers present.
- As soon as Carrillo gets out of his car to record, an agent in the Impala rolls down the window: "What do you want?"
- Agents begin driving in slow circles around the Carrillos, pausing to exchange insults. They take pictures of Carrillo and his car.
- An agent in the Impala tells Carrillo to come over and pulls out mace, pointing it at him through the open window. Carrillo stays by his car. Agents drive away.

### January 9, 2026 — Sam's Club Parking Lot, American Boulevard, Bloomington
- **~5:15 PM** - Carrillo and his wife hear about ICE activity at Sam's Club off American Boulevard. They arrive and see agents talking to an elderly woman who is trying to show them papers she has in her hand. No other observers are present.
- Carrillo gets out to record, standing approximately 10 feet from the agents.
- **Pepper spray** - Within seconds, what Carrillo believes is the same maroon Dodge Ram from January 7 speeds up next to him. An agent jumps out: "Back the fuck up, you're impeding." Carrillo responds: "No, you back the fuck up, I'm walking away." The agent sprays him directly in the eyes with pepper spray.
- **Second spray** - The spray feels cold on his eyeballs, then burns. Nose runs, eyes water. He hunches over, trying to wipe his eyes. When he looks up, someone sprays him again in the eyes. He cannot see who because he cannot see anything. He walks away and asks someone for water.
- **Threats to others** - Inside Sam's Club, another couple tells Carrillo agents threatened them with arrest for honking their horns. Carrillo's wife was threatened with arrest for blowing a whistle. Agents told her to "stop making all that noise."
- **Elderly woman released** - The woman agents had detained in the parking lot is later released because she had been unlawfully detained and had lawful status, according to her family.

## Legal Action

### Tincher v. Noem (Case 0:25-cv-04669-KMM-DTS)
Carrillo's declaration was filed January 12, 2026, as Document 60. The apparent retaliation pattern — the same vehicle from the January 7 encounter targeting him two days later — supports plaintiffs' claims that agents are targeting and retaliating against observers.

## Context
The apparent connection between the two incidents is significant. On January 7, agents in a maroon Dodge Ram with Iowa plates photographed Carrillo and his car and pointed mace at him. Two days later, at a different location on the same road, Carrillo recognized what he believed was the same maroon Dodge Ram moments before being pepper sprayed. If the vehicle was indeed the same, this suggests agents identified and targeted a known observer rather than responding to any immediate threat. Carrillo was standing 10 feet away and walking away when he was sprayed.
