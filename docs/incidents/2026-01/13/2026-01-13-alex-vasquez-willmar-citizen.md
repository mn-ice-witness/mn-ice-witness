# U.S. Citizen Handcuffed at Willmar Convenience Store, Driven Away, Released in Parking Lot

## Summary
Alex Vasquez, a 34-year-old U.S. citizen from Willmar, was approached by ICE agents at a convenience store on 1st Street on a Monday afternoon. Agents requested identification, then handcuffed him and placed him in their vehicle. They drove him approximately one mile before releasing him in a fast food restaurant parking lot.
