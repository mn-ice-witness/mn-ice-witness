# Army Veteran William Vermie Detained 8 Hours for Observing ICE Arrest

## Summary
Combat-wounded Army veteran William Vermie was detained for approximately eight hours after observing an ICE arrest from a public sidewalk in south Minneapolis. Vermie, a Purple Heart recipient who served in Iraq, was denied access to a phone and attorney throughout his detention at the Whipple Federal Building. He was released without charges.
