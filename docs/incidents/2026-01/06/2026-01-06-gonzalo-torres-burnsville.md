# Chilean Man Agrees to Voluntary Departure, Offers to Pay Own Airfare — Still Stuck in Louisiana Jail Five Weeks Later

## Summary
Gonzalo Torres, a Chilean national living in Burnsville, was arrested by ICE on January 6. He signed a voluntary departure agreement on January 8 and asked to buy his own plane ticket home, but was refused. Five weeks and three cancelled government flights later, he remains detained at Richwood Correctional Center in Louisiana, where he has described bathrooms overflowing with excrement and eating off the floor.

## Timeline
- **Jan 6** - Arrested by ICE in Burnsville.
- **Jan 8** - Signed voluntary departure agreement. Asked to buy his own plane ticket to Chile; request denied.
- **Jan 15** - Shipped to El Paso, Texas. First government-organized flight cancelled.
- **Jan 25** - Transferred to Richwood Correctional Center in Monroe, Louisiana. Second flight cancelled.
- **Jan 28** - Third flight scheduled; also cancelled.
- **Feb 6** - Still detained at Richwood with no clear timeline for departure.
