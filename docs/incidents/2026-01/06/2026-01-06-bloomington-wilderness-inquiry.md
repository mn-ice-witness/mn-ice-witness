# Man Seeks Refuge in Bloomington Office During 6-Hour ICE Standoff

## Updates
- **2026-02-08**: Added CNN video coverage identifying the man as Aldrin Maldonado. Corrected standoff duration from 2 hours to 6 hours per CNN reporting. Added quotes from Maldonado and girlfriend. Added YouTube source.

## Summary
Aldrin Maldonado, a man being pursued by ICE agents, fled on foot and took refuge inside the Wilderness Inquiry office building in Bloomington. Pleading with office workers — "It's ICE. I have family here, please" — he was allowed to remain in the vestibule while staff denied ICE entry, citing lack of a judicial warrant. After a six-hour standoff with negotiations through an interpreter, Maldonado voluntarily surrendered after ICE presented a felony arrest warrant. He was taken to the Whipple Building and later transferred to Texas. His girlfriend later said: "It's hard when the kids ask where he is."

## What Happened
Maldonado was stopped by ICE agents while driving to work near the Walmart in Bloomington. He fled on foot and ran into the Wilderness Inquiry building, where staff allowed him to stay in the vestibule. The building's staff consulted legal counsel about what to do while Maldonado remained visible to ICE agents through the glass.

Bloomington Police arrived but their involvement was "limited to helping maintain the peace." ICE agents were denied entry as they initially lacked a judicial warrant for the private building.

Video captured by CNN shows Maldonado's emotional pleas to office workers as he entered the building, as well as the extended standoff that followed. The standoff lasted approximately six hours before Maldonado surrendered.
