# Ecuadorian Asylum Seeker Detained on School Run; Breastfeeding Infant Left Behind

## Summary
Yulexi Loor Tacuri, 27, an Ecuadorian asylum seeker who fled domestic violence in 2021, was detained by ICE in early January while driving home after dropping her 9-year-old son at school. Federal vehicles surrounded her car. She was sent to a Texas detention center and fitted with an ankle monitor. Because the arrest prevented her from breastfeeding her 1-year-old daughter — a U.S. citizen — she developed an infection. Her immigration attorney filed a habeas petition, which a federal judge granted. She was released after more than a week and returned to Minnesota, but as of mid-February still wore the ankle monitor and was afraid to leave her home.
