# Asylum Seeker Hospitalized After Wife Says ICE Rammed His Car Into Telephone Pole

## Summary
Christian Salamanca, a Colombian asylum seeker with a valid visa and work permit, was hospitalized with serious injuries after his wife says ICE agents chased him and rammed his car into a telephone pole at McDonough Recreation Center in St. Paul. Nicole Salamanca says her husband called her in a panic around 11:15 a.m. on January 26 while driving to work, saying someone was following him. At her suggestion, he drove to the recreation center hoping it would be a safer location. According to Nicole, as Christian turned into the parking lot, ICE agents rammed his car from behind, pushing it into a telephone pole. Photos show the vehicle crushed between the pole and signage. Christian managed to exit the car and ran inside the building to hide.

## Wife's Account
Nicole Salamanca says her husband was on his way to work when ICE agents began following him. He called her around 11:15 a.m. in a panic. She advised him to drive to McDonough Recreation Center, thinking it would be safer.

As Christian turned into the parking lot, Nicole says ICE agents "rammed Christian's car from behind," pushing it into a telephone pole. Photos from the scene show the vehicle crushed and wedged between the pole and signage outside the recreation center.

After the crash, Christian managed to get out and run inside the building to hide.

## Questions Raised
Nicole says her husband has a valid visa, a pending asylum case, a work permit, and no criminal history. She does not understand why ICE would be pursuing him.

KARE 11 reached out to ICE for comment but had not received a response at time of publication.
