# Observer Tackled and Pepper-Sprayed Directly in Eye Minutes After Pretti Killing

## Summary
Clayton Kelly, 25, a bus mechanic for Minneapolis Metro Transit, arrived at the scene on Nicollet Avenue less than 40 minutes after Alex Pretti was fatally shot on January 24. Federal agents tackled him face-first onto the sidewalk, piled on top of him — with one placing a knee on the site of prior spinal fusion surgery — and put a pepper spray nozzle behind his glasses and sprayed directly into his left eye. He was detained at the Whipple Federal Building for approximately eight hours. Methodist Hospital documented his injuries as "assault-related." Kelly had also witnessed the Sosa-Celis shooting ten days earlier; his account contradicted DHS's official narrative. He filed a sworn declaration in Tincher v. Noem. He now says he is often afraid to leave his house.
