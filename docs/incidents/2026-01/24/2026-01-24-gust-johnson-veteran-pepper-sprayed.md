# 76-Year-Old Marine Veteran Pepper Sprayed Three Times, Detained 10 Hours at Pretti Scene

## Summary
Gust Johnson, a 76-year-old Marine Corps combat veteran and mostly retired nurse, was pepper sprayed three times by ICE agents, tackled, and detained for approximately 10 hours at the scene of the Alex Pretti shooting. He was bleeding from his elbow and missing his hearing aid upon release. His phone was seized and kept at the Whipple Building as "evidence" of an alleged assault on a federal agent, a charge Johnson disputes.
