# Plainclothes Federal Agent Brandishes Gun at Resident Recording ICE Vehicles at Plymouth Hotel

## Summary
Shane Mirkkovitch was recording federal immigration officer vehicles in the Residence Inn parking lot in Plymouth when a plainclothes individual approached and demanded to know who he was. The individual then retrieved a firearm from his vehicle and brandished it while repeating the demands. Mirkkovitch ordered the person to put the weapon away and recorded the encounter on video. He filed complaints with Plymouth Police and the hotel.
