# An Estimated 50,000 to 100,000 March in Subzero Temperatures in "Day of Truth & Freedom" Protest

## Summary
An estimated 50,000 to 100,000 people marched through downtown Minneapolis in subzero temperatures in the "ICE Out of Minnesota: Day of Truth & Freedom" protest against Operation Metro Surge. The attendance range is an organizer estimate reported by multiple outlets; no independent count was conducted. More than 700 businesses across Minnesota closed in solidarity in what was described as a general strike. The march began at The Commons near U.S. Bank Stadium and ended with a rally at Target Center.
