# 50,000 March in Subzero Temperatures in Largest Minnesota Protest

## Summary
An estimated 50,000 to 100,000 people marched through downtown Minneapolis in approximately -20 degree temperatures in the "Day of Truth & Freedom" protest against Operation Metro Surge. The march was the largest protest in Minnesota history. Hundreds of businesses closed in solidarity in what was described as the first general strike in the United States in 80 years. The scale of the response signaled that public opinion had decisively turned against the federal operation.
