# Woman Arrested at Oak Terraces After Challenging ICE Presence on Private Property

## Summary
A Rochester woman was arrested by ICE agents at Oak Terraces apartments on January 23, 2026 after allegedly telling agents they did not have permission to be on private property. The arrest came with sharply conflicting accounts from witnesses and ICE about what occurred, including whether ICE rammed the woman's car or an "agitator" vehicle struck an ICE vehicle.

## Witness Accounts

### Rep. Andy Smith (DFL-Rochester)
In an Instagram video, Smith reported that ICE agents arrested the woman at Oak Terraces because she had told them they did not have permission to be on private property. Smith also said ICE agents rammed the woman's car before arresting her.

### Live-Streamed Video
Video from the scene showed a crowd yelling "You just hit her car!" and blowing whistles at ICE agents as they loaded the woman into a van and drove away.

## Official Accounts

### ICE Statement (via Rochester Police)
In what Rochester police communications coordinator Amanda Grayson called "not typical" communication, an ICE agent contacted the department Friday afternoon claiming:
- "Agitators" boxed in ICE agents at Oak Terraces
- An "agitator vehicle" hit an ICE vehicle
- Agents made an arrest for obstruction

### Rochester Police Department
RPD stated they are "not involved in these federal operations" and that ICE has historically not communicated with Rochester law enforcement about enforcement activities.
