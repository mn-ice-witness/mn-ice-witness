# Federal Agents Pull Over Two Anoka-Hennepin School Vans Carrying Students Near Dayton

## Summary
Federal agents stopped two Anoka-Hennepin School District vans transporting students near the Dayton, Minnesota city border during early morning hours. The van drivers — described as Native American and Asian by State Sen. Jim Abeler — followed established safety procedures before continuing their routes to school. Superintendent Cory McIntyre disclosed the incidents in a January 27 message to families, along with related incidents of ICE vehicles parked near Two Rivers Education Center in Anoka and near district bus stops. The exact date of the stops is not reported but occurred between January 12 and January 27. The incident was recorded on school district video.

## Timeline
- **Jan 12** - Superintendent McIntyre sends first message to families stating no ICE activity has occurred at district schools.
- **Between Jan 12 and Jan 27** - Federal agents stop two district vans carrying students near Dayton city border during early morning hours. Drivers follow safety protocols; vans continue to school.
- **Between Jan 12 and Jan 27** - ICE vehicles parked near Two Rivers Education Center at Franklin in Anoka. District staff approach agents and request they relocate; agents comply.
- **Jan 27** - McIntyre discloses the incidents in a second message to families. Parents of affected students notified; district investigating.
- **Feb 2** - State Sen. Jim Abeler (R-Anoka) sends letter to border czar Tom Homan, President Trump, and DHS Secretary Noem requesting reassignment of 500+ agents from Minnesota, citing the van stops.

## District Response
Anoka-Hennepin School District protocols include:
- Exterior doors locked during school hours
- Judicial warrants required for ICE access to students (administrative warrants insufficient)
- All warrants reviewed by district legal counsel
- Building "hold" protocol keeping students in classrooms during ICE incidents
- Parents contacted immediately if ICE agents with valid judicial warrants access a school
- Resource website: ahschools.us/immigration

## Official Response

**State Sen. Jim Abeler (R-Anoka):**
Described the van drivers as Native American and Asian, underscoring that legal residents and citizens were being stopped. Requested ICE reassign 500+ agents from Minnesota, citing "countless wrongful detentions of U.S. citizens and legal non-citizens."
