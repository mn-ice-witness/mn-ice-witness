# U.S. Citizen Arrested Twice by ICE in St. Cloud After Mistaken Identity

## Summary

Mohamed Abdirizak Jama, a 36-year-old U.S. citizen who has held citizenship for 15 years, was arrested by ICE agents in St. Cloud on January 20 after they mistook him for another person. Court records confirm the mistaken identity. Frightened by unmarked vehicles chasing him, Jama tried to drive away before agents boxed him in. He was taken to the Whipple Federal Building at Fort Snelling and released. Approximately eight days later, he was arrested again on charges of interfering with a federal officer stemming from the first encounter.
