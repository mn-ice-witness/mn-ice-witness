# Man Arrested at Detroit Lakes Restaurant; Partner Documents on Video

## Summary
ICE agents arrested a man outside the China Buffet restaurant in Detroit Lakes. Grace Waawaate-Ikwe Roberts documented the arrest on video. The detained man was taken to the ICE Field Office at Fort Snelling.

## Sources
1. Detroit Lakes Online (Jan 12, 2026): [ICE officers arrest man at Detroit Lakes restaurant on Monday](https://www.dl-online.com/news/local/ice-officers-arrest-man-at-detroit-lakes-restaurant-on-monday)
2. Valley News Live (Jan 12, 2026): [ICE presence confirmed in Detroit Lakes](https://www.valleynewslive.com/2026/01/12/ice-presence-confirmed-detroit-lakes/)

## Affected Individual(s)
- **Relationship:** Partner of Grace Waawaate-Ikwe Roberts
- **Destination:** ICE Field Office, 1 Federal Drive, Fort Snelling

## What Happened
ICE agents arrested the man in the parking lot in front of the China Buffet restaurant, located in a mini-mall at 317 Frazee Street. Roberts documented the arrest and was told by an ICE agent where he would be taken.

## Broader ICE Activity in Detroit Lakes
Following the arrest:
- ICE agents were reported at multiple locations in Detroit Lakes, including Walmart.
- Don Pablo's Mexican Restaurant temporarily closed due to ICE activity in the area.

## Context
This arrest occurred during what DHS Secretary Kristi Noem called the "largest immigration operation ever" in Minnesota. It came five days after the fatal shooting of Renee Good, amid heightened tensions statewide.
