# Minnesota Mother Detained 30 Days at Fort Bliss; Son Serves in Army National Guard

## Summary
Lidia Escobar Maya, 43, a mother of three U.S. citizen children who had lived in the United States for 23 years, was detained by ICE on Jan. 12 outside her home. Her 24-year-old son, who serves in the Army National Guard, had filed a military parole-in-place application to help adjust her status before she was detained. She was held for 30 days at Camp East Montana at Fort Bliss in El Paso, where she described being zip-tied, shackled, and housed in a tent camp with wet bedding and limited food. She was released Feb. 10 with an ankle monitor.
