# 50+ ICE Agents Swarm Somali Mall in St. Cloud; Chemical Irritants Used on Protesters

## Summary
More than 50 ICE agents descended on Star City Mall, a strip mall with numerous Somali-owned businesses, to arrest one man leaving a deli. Hundreds of protesters gathered and blocked ICE vehicles, leading to a tense standoff. Agents deployed chemical irritants on the crowd. At least four people were detained: the ICE target and three U.S. citizen observers/bystanders who were held for 8-9+ hours at the Whipple Federal Building before being released without charges. The incident came two days after Somali business owners at the same location successfully shouted ICE agents into leaving.

## What Happened

### The Arrest
ICE agents detained **Yonis Mohamed Abdullahi**, an Amazon employee, as he was coming out of a deli at Star City Mall around midday on January 12, 2026. According to Sahan Journal, Abdullahi had valid work authorization and does not appear on DHS's "Worst of the Worst" list of individuals with criminal records.

### Community Response
- People started gathering immediately after witnessing the arrest.
- Local Somali news station XIDIG TV posted live video, drawing more people to the scene.
- Hundreds of protesters arrived and blocked ICE vehicles in the parking lot.
- State Sen. Aric Putnam acted as shield between protesters and agents, urging calm.

### Use of Force
When protesters refused to allow ICE vehicles to leave, agents deployed chemical irritant on the crowd. XIDIG TV journalist Mohamed Jama reported witnessing agents "shoving and pepper-spraying protesters and even bystanders."

### Two Days Earlier (Jan 10)
In the same parking lot, Somali business owners and patrons confronted ICE agents so forcefully that the federal officers drove away without making arrests. Video of this incident went viral.

## Arrests and Detentions

### ICE Target
- **Yonis Mohamed Abdullahi** - Amazon employee with valid work authorization; arrested leaving deli

### U.S. Citizens Detained as Observers/Bystanders

**Alice Valentine** (25, U.S. citizen)
- Co-owner of Valentine's Salon in St. Cloud
- Arrived after learning of ICE presence through Signal group chat
- Pepper sprayed, slammed to ground, and handcuffed after telling agents not to touch her girlfriend
- Transported 80+ miles to Whipple Federal Building
- Detained 9+ hours; released without charges
- Denied phone call; cell phone not returned
- Sustained sprained ankle

**Sofia Martin** (32, U.S. citizen)
- Co-owner of Valentine's Salon, Valentine's partner
- Pushed back by agent when approaching to ask about assault rifles
- Pepper sprayed, slammed to ground, handcuffed
- Reported agent pulled her hair and felt like they would break her arm
- Transported to Whipple Building; held 9+ hours
- ICE agent asked invasive questions about her genitalia and whether she'd had gender-confirmation surgery
- Released without charges; cell phone not returned

**Chase Benjamin** (U.S. citizen)
- Video shows ICE officer swinging baton at him
- Stated he was "just walking, trying to get through the crowd"
- Said "At this point, I'm being kidnapped"
- Transported to Whipple Federal Building
- Detained approximately 8 hours before release
- Considering legal action

## Context
The operation occurred as the Department of Homeland Security pledged to send 2,000+ immigration officers into Minnesota in what ICE called its "largest enforcement operation ever." The same day, Minnesota and the Twin Cities sued the Trump administration to halt the surge.
