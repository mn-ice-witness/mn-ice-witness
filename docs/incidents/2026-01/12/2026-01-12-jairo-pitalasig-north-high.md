# North High School Senior Detained at Construction Job, Deported to Ecuador

## Summary
Jairo Pitalasig, an 18-year-old senior at North High School in Minneapolis, was detained by ICE at his construction job on January 12. He had been working full-time to support his Ecuadorian family while they sought asylum. His father — who was in remission from cancer — had been detained the day before and sent to El Paso. Both were transferred to Texas. Jairo had missed one routine immigration court date, resulting in an in-absentia removal order. He ultimately accepted deportation to Ecuador.
