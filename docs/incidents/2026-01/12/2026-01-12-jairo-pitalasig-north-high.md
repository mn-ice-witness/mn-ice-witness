# North High School Senior Detained at Construction Job, Deported to Ecuador

## Summary
Jairo Pitalasig, an 18-year-old senior at North High School in Minneapolis, was detained by ICE at his construction job on January 12. His father had been detained the day before. Both were transferred to El Paso, and Jairo ultimately accepted deportation to Ecuador.
