# Two Observers Sprayed Directly in Face with Chemical Irritant by ICE Agent in Mankato

## Summary
Two observers were pepper sprayed directly in the face (chemical irritant) by a uniformed federal agent on Monday afternoon in Mankato. State Sen. Aric Putnam placed himself between the crowd and federal agents during the "really intense confrontation."

## Sources
1. Facebook Video (Jan 12, 2026): [Women sprayed with chemical irritant](https://www.facebook.com/reel/3898161177152426)
2. Star Tribune (Jan 13, 2026): [ICE activity, increasing in rural Minnesota and cities outside metro, increases anxiety](https://www.startribune.com/with-ice-surge-spreading-across-state-rumors-and-anxiety-grow-in-greater-minnesota/601562255)

## Affected Individual(s)
- Two observers (one identified as Ava Corey-Gruenes)
- Sprayed with chemical irritant

## What Happened
According to video posted online, two observers were sprayed with a chemical irritant by a uniformed man on Monday afternoon in Old Town Mankato near Riverfront Drive. Agents also deployed chemical irritant and one swung a baton at a protester.

## ICE Activity in Mankato
- ICE contingent spotted along Madison Avenue near Walmart and Sam's Club on Saturday
- Agents spotted at a house near Mankato East High School
- Agents eventually left after local law enforcement and elected officials worked to calm the crowd

## State Senator Statement

### Sen. Aric Putnam (DFL)
Placed himself between the crowd and federal agents on Monday: "a stressful moment, a really intense confrontation."

## Regional Context
Nearby St. James (approximately 39 miles southwest of Mankato) is home to a Smithfield Foods meat processing plant that employs many immigrant workers. Tension is high throughout the region.
