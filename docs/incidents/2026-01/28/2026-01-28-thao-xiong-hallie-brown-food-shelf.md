# Food Shelf Volunteer Thao Xiong Detained at Hallie Q. Brown Community Center

## Summary

Thao Xiong, a Hmong community activist and food shelf volunteer, was detained by federal agents on January 28, 2026, as he arrived at the Hallie Q. Brown Community Center in St. Paul to help unload a weekly food delivery. Xiong served time in prison and was released in 2016, after which he worked to turn his life around by volunteering and working with local nonprofits. He is the father of a young daughter.

- **Name:** Thao Xiong
- **Ethnicity:** Hmong
- **Status:** Has removal order from February 23, 2010; father of a young daughter

## Key Details

Federal agents were waiting in the parking lot of the community center. According to video footage, agents approached Xiong, handcuffed him, and placed him into one of three black SUVs despite his attempts to show documentation. Benny Roberts, director of the Hallie Q. Brown Community Center, confirmed a volunteer had been detained.

DHS stated that Xiong has a removal order dated February 23, 2010, and that he would remain in custody pending removal.
