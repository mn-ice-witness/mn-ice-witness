# Four Torture Survivors with Pending Asylum Cases Detained by ICE

## Summary
The Center for Victims of Torture, a St. Paul nonprofit, reported that four of their clients - all torture survivors with pending asylum cases, valid work permits, and no criminal records - were detained by ICE. One was reportedly tackled in the snow in front of coworkers while agents laughed. The center warned of severe mental health impacts on their clients due to ICE enforcement.
