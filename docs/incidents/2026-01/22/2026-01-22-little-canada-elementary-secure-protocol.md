# Little Canada Elementary Goes to Secure Protocol Due to ICE in Parking Lot

## Summary
Little Canada Elementary School in Roseville Area Schools implemented "secure" protocol after ICE agents were observed in vehicles in the school parking lot during school hours. The school locked all entrances while classes continued normally inside.
