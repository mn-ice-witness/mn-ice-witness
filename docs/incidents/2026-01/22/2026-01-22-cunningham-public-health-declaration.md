# Minnesota Health Commissioner: Operation Metro Surge Causing Measurable Public Health Damage Statewide

## Summary
Dr. Brooke Cunningham, Commissioner of the Minnesota Department of Health, filed a sworn declaration on January 22, 2026, in State of Minnesota v. Noem (Case 26-cv-190), documenting the measurable impact of Operation Metro Surge on public health services across Minnesota. The declaration details a 40% increase in no-shows at federally qualified health centers, dramatic drops in HIV testing among Latino populations, cancellation of vaccine clinics, families isolating to the point of storing garbage inside their homes, and non-white MDH field staff being followed by federal law enforcement vehicles while driving state-issued cars.
