# Paul Johnson Beaten at Gas Station; Bondi Posts "Trophy" Hospital Photo

## Updates
- **Feb 11** - [Star Tribune reports DOJ walking back assault charges to misdemeanors](https://www.startribune.com/after-assault-allegations-doj-walks-back-charges-against-ice-protesters/601578511) in roughly 20 cases, including Johnson's; former federal prosecutors say the move indicates the evidence does not support the original felony allegations. Charges against Johnson remained pending as of early June.

## Summary
North Minneapolis resident Paul Johnson, a U.S. citizen, was detained by federal agents at the Super USA Deli parking lot on Jan. 22, 2026, sustaining a traumatic brain injury and a shoulder injury that his attorney said will require reconstructive surgery. While Johnson was hospitalized, agents stood over his bed taking photographs on personal cellphones; one image — showing him shackled with his arm in a sling — was posted on X by U.S. Attorney General Pam Bondi alongside a caption labeling 16 Minnesota defendants "rioters." His attorney says U.S. Marshals Service policy forbids posing prisoners for photos except for identification.
