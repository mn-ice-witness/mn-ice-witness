# St. Peter Police Chief Intervenes, Prevents Federal Agents From Arresting ICE Observer

## Summary
In what is believed to be the first time local police in Minnesota intervened in a federal law enforcement action during Operation Metro Surge, the St. Peter police chief prevented federal agents from arresting a local resident who was observing ICE enforcement. Three federal agents were seen pointing guns at the ICE observer near Jefferson Park in St. Peter on Thursday, January 30.

## What Happened
- An ICE observer was near Jefferson Park in St. Peter when three federal agents pointed guns at them
- The St. Peter police chief intervened and prevented the arrest
- This is believed to be the first time local police in Minnesota intervened in a federal law enforcement action during Operation Metro Surge
