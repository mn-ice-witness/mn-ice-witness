# U.S. Citizen Zip-Tied and Detained Near Nicollet Mall; Agents Mocked Her Hijab

## Summary
A Somali woman born in Edina, a U.S. citizen, was zip-tied by federal agents while running errands near Nicollet Mall in downtown Minneapolis on Dec. 3. Her cousin told FOX 9 that agents mocked her hijab and touched her inappropriately. She was taken to the Sherburne County Jail in Elk River and held for more than 24 hours before being released after her husband showed her passport card. ICE acting director of public affairs Tom Wackman denied that agents arrested the woman or touched her inappropriately.

## Disputed Account
The woman's cousin told FOX 9 that agents mocked her hijab, saying "What could you be hiding under your hijab?" and "This would be the wrong attire to try and run away from us in." He also alleged agents touched her inappropriately.

ICE acting director of public affairs Tom Wackman told FOX 9 that "agents never arrested the woman or touched her inappropriately." Immigration attorney Evangeline Dhawan-Maloney responded: "We have reason to doubt the narrative the administration is pushing."
