# ICE Observer Boxed In by Four Vehicles, Threatened with Arrest

## Summary
Lucia Webb, a 31-year-old Powderhorn neighborhood resident and nonprofit operations director, was boxed in by four ICE vehicles and surrounded by approximately five masked, armed agents after following two ICE vehicles from south Minneapolis to the Metro Transit Park and Ride near the Whipple Federal Building. Agents threatened her with arrest for "impeding officers," accused her of breaking traffic laws she had not broken, and insulted her appearance before leaving.

## Affected Individual(s)
- **Name:** Lucia Webb
- **Age:** 31
- **Citizenship:** U.S. Citizen
- **Residence:** Powderhorn neighborhood, Minneapolis (since 2016)
- **Occupation:** Operations director at a local nonprofit

## Timeline
- **Afternoon, Dec 3** - Webb monitors neighborhood Signal chat for ICE activity. This is her second day doing ICE watch.
- Hears ICE may be near Portland Ave. and 42nd St. in south Minneapolis. Drives to location.
- Spots vehicles with very dark tinted windows and Virginia license plates. Other observers on scene confirm the vehicles contain ICE agents.
- ICE vehicles pull away. Webb follows them west on 42nd St. toward 35W, staying a few car lengths behind at normal driving distance. She does not run red lights or break traffic laws.
- ICE vehicles get on 35W southbound. Webb follows at a reasonable speed, not faster than traffic.
- Vehicles exit onto Hwy 62. Webb realizes they are near the Whipple Building.
- An agent is standing in the middle of the road as if directing traffic. Webb slows and stops, unsure what to do. The agent appears to wave her through.
- Webb follows one ICE vehicle into the Metro Transit Park and Ride parking lot across from the Whipple Building.
- **Boxed in** - Four ICE vehicles surround her: one in front, the rest to the sides. Approximately five masked and armed agents walk toward her car and surround it. Webb starts filming.
- The closest agent accuses her of chasing ICE vehicles, breaking traffic laws, and running red lights. He claims they have her on video breaking the law. Webb had not broken any traffic laws.
- Agent tells her she is "impeding officers" and threatens arrest if she doesn't stop following them. Webb tells the agent she is trying to protect her neighbors.
- Agent: "You can not do this." Webb: "Yes, I can. Yes, I can."
- Webb asks agents if they are embarrassed to be kidnapping people like "Nazis." They reply: "Not really."
- Agents insult and mock her, crudely insulting her appearance.
- Agents get back in their vehicles and leave.
- **After** - Webb stays in her parked car and cries. "I just felt so sad and really shaken. You're like, 'They can't do anything to me,' and it feels like they can when you're in that moment — yeah, I felt scared."
- Webb turns to neighborhood Signal chats for support. Others respond with invitations for tea and candy.

## Next Day: December 4, 2025
Webb returned to observing the very next day, this time in a friend's car. At around 4:00 PM, Webb and her friend were driving on Park Avenue following ICE vehicles with Texas plates as part of a group of observers. After turning onto 31st St., another car pulled a U-turn and stopped in the middle of the road, blocking observers' vehicles. One or two masked agents got out and aimed a gun at people observing on foot and at the cars. As one agent returned to his vehicle, he smacked one of the observer cars and the front end of his gun appeared to go inside that car's window.

## Affected Individual Statement

On continuing: "It just reminds me that people can and will show up, and that there's a lot of us. There's more of us than there are of them."

"I have a meeting later this afternoon, so I'm not going to be out driving on the streets, but other people will be — that feels powerful."

## Legal Action

### Tincher v. Noem (Case 0:25-cv-04669-KMM-DTS)
Webb is a named plaintiff in ACLU of Minnesota lawsuit filed December 17, 2025, challenging ICE's treatment of observers and protesters. The suit seeks a preliminary injunction limiting agents' use of firearms, chemical irritants, and arrests of citizens not resisting dispersal orders.

## Context
The incident occurred on December 3, 2025 — the same day as the [Hola Arepa warrantless entry attempt](/entry/2025-12-03-hola-arepa-warrantless-entry) and just two days after Operation Metro Surge began. Webb was part of a rapidly growing network of neighborhood ICE watchers coordinating through Signal chats, many of which originated during the 2020 unrest following the killing of George Floyd.
