# 20-Year U.S. Citizen Arrested Near Karmel Mall, Subjected to Racist Statements by ICE Agent

## Updates
- **Feb 8** - Corrected date from Dec 16 to Dec 15 per ACLU complaint. This incident occurred during the same confrontation as the [pregnant woman dragged through snow](/entry/2025-12-15-karmel-mall-pregnant-woman). Added ACLU complaint details, Noor's subsequent January 12 arrest in St. Cloud, and January 29 federal charges. Changed status to under-investigation.

## Summary
Abdikadir Abdi Noor, a 43-year-old Fridley resident and trucking company owner who has been a U.S. citizen for approximately 20 years, was tackled and arrested by ICE agents on Pillsbury Avenue near Karmel Mall while trying to calm a crowd during a confrontation in which agents detained multiple people and kneeled on a woman's back for approximately 30 minutes. Noor had been asserting his constitutional rights and advising others of theirs. He was driven to the Whipple Building at approximately 85 mph without a seatbelt, shackled in a cell, and subjected to racist statements echoing Secretary Noem's rhetoric about Somali immigrants. He was released without charges at approximately 6:00 PM. Noor was subsequently arrested again in St. Cloud on January 12, 2026, and on January 29 was one of 16 people charged by the U.S. Attorney's Office with "violently assaulting federal officers and property."

## Timeline

### December 15, 2025 — Karmel Mall Area, Minneapolis
- **Afternoon** - Noor is with his wife Farhiya and a friend after lunch at Marhaba restaurant (Nicollet Ave. and 28th St.). They head to Karmel Mall for coffee.
- Driving on Pillsbury Avenue, nearly at the mall, Noor notices cars behind him. He pulls over, initially thinking it's police.
- Agents exit the vehicle — plain clothes, military vests, masks. Noor realizes they are ICE.
- Noor gets out and asserts his rights: "I'm not going to let you do this. I'm not going to show you anything or tell you anything."
- Agents do not approach Noor. Instead they go to another car boxed in behind his — two Latino occupants surrounded by four agents in vests and masks.
- Noor calls out to the Latino people: "You don't have to show them anything. Don't roll down your window or unlock your door!"
- Another woman arrives and also begins telling people about their rights. A crowd gathers.
- The woman is grabbed by an agent and thrown to the ground. An agent kneels on her back. Someone in the crowd says she is pregnant. The crowd yells at agents to let her go. Noor also tells agents to let her go.
- While the woman is pinned down, agents break the windows of the car with the Latino occupants and pull them out. Noor does not see an arrest warrant.
- The woman remains face down in the snow with an agent kneeling on her back for approximately 30 minutes. The crowd continues to tell ICE to stop.
- Noor attempts to calm the crowd and prevent them from throwing snow: "I told the people not to throw things, we needed to save the lady, and we needed to save the agent from himself. We needed to be peaceful."
- The agent kneeling on the woman drags her violently toward his car. Noor follows, trying to keep people back and calm while telling agents what they're doing is wrong.
- After backup arrives and the woman leaves, agents turn on Noor. One says something like "let's get this guy." They advance, grab him, throw him to the ground, and handcuff him, causing bruises on his knees and head.
- **~1:40 PM** - Agents put Noor in a car and leave the area. The bald agent driving speeds at approximately 85 mph on 35W toward the Whipple Building. He refuses to let Noor put on a seatbelt.
- **At Whipple Building** - Agent looks at Noor's passport and says to another officer: "They all come here fraudulently. 50% are here fraudulently" — echoing Secretary Noem's rhetoric at the start of Operation Metro Surge. The agent continues: "Somalis drained Minnesota" and "Somalis should go back home."
- Noor is shackled and left alone in a cell. Eventually read his rights, which he exercises.
- **~6:00 PM** - Released without charge, paperwork, or explanation.

### January 12, 2026 — Star City Mall, St. Cloud
Noor was among those detained when approximately 80 ICE agents descended on the 33rd Avenue Somali Mall (Star City Mall) in St. Cloud. A two-hour standoff erupted between hundreds of residents and federal agents in the parking lot. Per Sahan Journal, Noor was accused of throwing ice at officers after they pepper sprayed him. He was released with visible bruises on his face. A photo shown at a February 4 press conference showed bruising on one cheek.

### January 29, 2026 — Federal Charges
The U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of Minnesota charged Noor as one of 16 defendants with "violently assaulting federal officers and property." The DOJ alleges Noor "threw a brick at Immigration and Customs Enforcement Officers" and that when officers attempted to arrest him, "he fled and, once caught, attempted to avoid being handcuffed by grappling with the Officers." Community organizers and Sahan Journal reported that Noor threw ice, not a brick, at agents — and only after agents had pepper sprayed him.

## Affected Individual Statement

### Abdikadir Abdi Noor
"If I were these people, I would figure out another way to detain people they need to detain — set an appointment, something orderly. But they're just trying to make our cities look chaotic, like World War III. People call me from Europe, from Africa, asking me 'Is this really America? It looks like a third world country.' The whole time I was there at the scene, I just wanted to tell the government that what they were doing to the woman, to the Latinos, to all of us, is wrong. I just wanted to tell people what their rights are in this country and under our Constitution."

## Legal Action

### Tincher v. Noem (Case 0:25-cv-04669-KMM-DTS)
Noor is a named plaintiff in ACLU of Minnesota lawsuit filed December 17, 2025, challenging ICE's treatment of observers and protesters. The suit seeks a preliminary injunction limiting agents' use of firearms, chemical irritants, and arrests of citizens not resisting dispersal orders.

### Federal Criminal Charges
Noor faces federal charges for allegedly assaulting federal officers. The complaint was signed by Special Agent Richard Berger, Homeland Security Investigations. The case is part of DOJ's "Operation Take Back America." A felony trial cannot be held on a complaint; a decision on seeking an indictment is pending.

## Context
This incident occurred during the same confrontation documented in the [pregnant woman dragged through snow](/entry/2025-12-15-karmel-mall-pregnant-woman) incident. Karmel Mall is the nation's first Somali shopping center and has been a frequent site of ICE activity. The agent's racist statements at the Whipple Building directly echoed Secretary Noem's false claim, made at the start of Operation Metro Surge, that 50% of Somali visas in Minnesota were "fraudulent" — a claim contradicted by USCIS's own investigation, which found roughly 0.5% of cases warranted referral.
