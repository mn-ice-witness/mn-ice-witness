# ICE Agent Fires Shots at Man During St. Paul Arrest After Being Struck by Vehicle

## Updates
- **2026-02-08**: Identified suspect as Juan Carlos Romero, a Cuban national who entered the U.S. via the CBP One app in 2024. Added detail that Romero bit an ICE agent. Added 3 new sources (Fox 9, MPR News, KATU). Added DHS/ICE statement and Tricia McLaughlin account.

## Summary
An ICE agent fired two shots at Juan Carlos Romero, a Cuban national, during an arrest attempt in St. Paul on December 21, 2025, after Romero struck two agents with his SUV. No one was hit by gunfire. Two ICE officers were hospitalized with non-life-threatening injuries. Romero, who entered the U.S. in 2024 through the Biden administration's CBP One app, allegedly refused to comply with a vehicle stop, struck one agent while driving away, rammed an ICE vehicle at his apartment complex striking a second agent, then attempted to flee on foot. During his apprehension, he bit an ICE agent. ICE blamed "sanctuary politicians" for creating conditions that led to the confrontation.

## Suspect
- **Name:** Juan Carlos Romero
- **Nationality:** Cuban national
- **Immigration status:** Entered U.S. in 2024 via the CBP One app (Biden administration program)
- **Status:** In ICE custody

## Timeline
- **~8:20 AM** - St. Paul Police called to 1300 block of Westminster Street for reports of shots fired.
- **Vehicle stop** - ICE agents identify themselves during a vehicle stop. Romero refuses to roll down his window. Agents warn they will break the window if he doesn't comply.
- **First collision** - Romero drives away, striking an ICE agent.
- **Parking lot** - Romero drives to the parking lot of his apartment complex, where agents stop him again and order him out.
- **Second collision** - Romero rams his SUV into an ICE vehicle, striking a second agent. He also hits two parked vehicles.
- **Shots fired** - The agent struck by the vehicle fires two "defensive shots"; no one is hit.
- **Third ram** - Romero rams another ICE vehicle.
- **Flight and arrest** - Romero exits his SUV and attempts to flee into his apartment. Agents bring him to the ground. During apprehension, he bites an agent.

## Injuries
- Two ICE officers hospitalized with non-life-threatening injuries
- Romero hospitalized for evaluation; not struck by gunfire

## Statements

### ICE
"This dangerous attempt to evade arrest comes after sanctuary politicians, like Governor Walz and other radical leftists, demeaned ICE and held events to help illegal aliens evade arrest. Attacks on ICE are up 1,150% and death threats up 8,000%. The two officers who sustained injuries are okay, but this could have gone sideways fast."

### Tricia McLaughlin, DHS Assistant Secretary for Public Affairs
Provided a detailed account to the Associated Press: agents identified themselves, the man refused to roll down his window, they warned they would break it, he drove off striking one agent, then rammed vehicles and a second agent at his apartment complex.

### St. Paul Police
Officers were not involved in the arrest or use of force. Called to the scene on a report of shots fired and confirmed ICE had fired the shots.

## Context
This occurred during Operation Metro Surge. Romero entered the U.S. in 2024 through the CBP One app, a Biden-era program that allowed migrants to schedule appointments at ports of entry. The incident is one of two ICE shooting incidents during Operation Metro Surge (the other being the [fatal shooting of Renee Good](/entry/2026-01-07-renee-good-shooting)).
