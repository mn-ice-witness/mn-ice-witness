# 13-Year-Old Boy Handcuffed, Thrown to Ground by ICE Agents

## Summary
ICE agents detained Jose Gomez outside an Eden Prairie apartment complex while his 13-year-old son Jose was handcuffed, allegedly thrown to the ground, and restrained by officers. Jose was later released but experienced medical complications including abnormal heartbeat that required hospitalization at St. Francis Regional Medical Center.
