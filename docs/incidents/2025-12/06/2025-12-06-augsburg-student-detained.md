# ICE Agents Point Weapons at Students During Campus Arrest at Augsburg University

## Summary
ICE agents detained undergraduate student Jesus Saucedo-Portillo in Parking Lot B at Augsburg University, a private campus in Minneapolis. University officials said agents pointed weapons at students and staff, including those watching from residence hall windows, and refused to produce a judicial warrant when asked. DHS disputed this account, claiming agents had a warrant and were obstructed by a university administrator.

## Affected Individual(s)
- **Name:** Jesus Saucedo-Portillo
- **Status:** Undergraduate student at Augsburg University
- **Citizenship:** Undocumented (per DHS claims)
- **Background:** DHS claims he is a registered sex offender with a DWI conviction; however, Star Tribune found no record of a DWI case in Minnesota court records, and his name does not appear in the national sex offender registry

## Timeline
- **~3:00 PM** - ICE agents in an unmarked vehicle follow Saucedo-Portillo into Parking Lot B adjacent to a residence hall.
- **Initial contact** - Armed, masked agents approach him as he gets into his vehicle.
- **Campus response** - Campus Public Safety and residence hall staff respond to the scene.
- **Warrant request** - An on-call administrator arrives and asks agents for a judicial warrant.
- **Warrant dispute** - University says agents stated they did NOT have a judicial warrant; DHS says they did have one.
- **Weapons drawn** - More agents arrive, pointing weapons at the crowd and pushing witnesses back; students watch from residence hall windows.
- **Obstruction claim** - DHS says administrator "ordered campus security to stand in front of the vehicle" to block ICE from leaving.
- **Detained** - Saucedo-Portillo is taken off campus.

## Official Accounts

### DHS/ICE Statement
DHS Assistant Secretary for Public Affairs Tricia McLaughlin released a statement disputing the university's account:

> "Officers HAD a warrant and told the University staff and administration that federal law supersedes any University policy... a University administrator attempted to obstruct the arrest and directed University security to stand between officers and an exit, preventing them from leaving."

DHS claimed Saucedo-Portillo is an "illegal alien," a "registered sex offender," and has a prior DWI conviction. They noted a 1,050% increase in assaults on officers nationwide.

**Fact check:** Star Tribune found no record of a DWI case under his name in Minnesota court records. His name does not appear in the national sex offender registry. (Minnesota only requires Level 3 sex offenders to register publicly; lower-level offenses may not appear.)

### University Statement
President Paul Pribbenow called the arrest "illegal" and "unacceptable, dangerous, and profoundly disturbing."

Provost Paula O'Loughlin said agents were "pointing weapons at the crowd and pushing witnesses back."

**University response:**
- All academic buildings switched to keycard access only
- Students who felt unsafe were allowed to remain in campus housing through holidays
- University announced plans to review footage and explore legal action
- Distributed whistles with coded signals (multiple short blows = ICE presence; continuous = detention)

### Local Officials
**Mayor Jacob Frey** signed an executive order restricting ICE use of city property.

**Police Chief Brian O'Hara** confirmed Minneapolis Police will not cooperate with ICE operations.

## Witness Accounts
Students recorded video from residence hall windows showing at least two masked, armed men with rifles near the residence hall entrance and unmarked SUVs leaving the parking lot.

## Legal Context
According to the National Immigration Law Center:
- A **judicial warrant** (signed by a federal judge) is required for officers to enter private property or detain someone there.
- Most ICE arrest warrants are **administrative documents** signed by immigration officials, NOT judicial warrants.
- Administrative warrants do NOT authorize entry into private areas without consent.
- Augsburg is a **private university** - the parking lot, though ungated, is private property.

## Related Events
Following the arrest, Augsburg received two bomb threats from the same anonymous person:
- **December 10:** Lindell Library evacuated
- **December 11:** Anderson Music Hall evacuated
- No explosives found; disrupted final exams
