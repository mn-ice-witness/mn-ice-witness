# Brooklyn Park Cook Arrested Outside Workplace; Restaurant Remains Closed

## Summary
Jorge Vergara Rubio, an Ecuadorian cook, was arrested by ICE outside his employer Crumbs and Coffee in Brooklyn Park. His employer claims Rubio was legally authorized to work at the time of arrest, though ICE states his employment authorization application was denied in September 2025. The restaurant remains closed following the arrest.

## Affected Individual(s)
- **Name:** Jorge Vergara Rubio
- **Origin:** Ecuador
- **Occupation:** Cook at Crumbs and Coffee
- **Entry:** November 2023 (per ICE)
- **Work Authorization:** Disputed (employer says authorized; ICE says application denied Sept 2025)

## Disputed Work Authorization
- **ICE claim:** Rubio "illegally entered the U.S. in Nov. 2023 and was RELEASED by the Biden administration." Employment authorization application denied September 2025.
- **Employer claim:** Charles Spies, owner of Crumbs and Coffee, stated Rubio "was legally authorized to work" at the time of arrest.
