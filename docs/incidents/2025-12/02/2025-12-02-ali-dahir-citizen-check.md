# Citizen Check: U.S. Citizen Detained 30 Minutes Outside Apartment Despite Showing Passport

## Summary
Ali Dahir, a 44-year-old Somali American U.S. citizen, was stopped by five ICE agents outside his Lake Street apartment building on December 2, 2025. Despite presenting his passport card and Minnesota driver's license, agents held him for 25-30 minutes in 19-degree weather, photographed his face without consent, and threatened to detain him for recording the encounter. Agents only left when media and neighbors gathered, telling each other "It's getting hot over here, let's go."

2. Facebook Photo (Dec 2, 2025): [Photo of encounter](https://www.facebook.com/photo.php?fbid=1278364750986218&set=a.636197348536298&id=100064381164299)

## Affected Individual(s)
- **Name:** Ali Dahir
- **Age:** 44
- **Citizenship:** U.S. Citizen (naturalized in 2006)
- **Ethnicity:** Somali American
- **Occupation:** Medical transport driver for Mas Transport, Inc.
- **Background:** Lived in U.S. since 2001; in Minnesota from 2001-2008, returned last year. No criminal record, never stopped by law enforcement before.

## Timeline
- **~1:30 p.m.** - Dahir leaves his Lake Street apartment with a friend to get food at Karmel Mall before a 2:30 p.m. work pickup.
- **Exiting building** - Sees ICE officers in building knocking on doors. Rides elevator with 5-6 officers without incident.
- **Outside building** - One officer approaches: "I need to talk to you." Five officers surround Dahir and his friend.
- **Questioning** - Officer says they are looking for someone who "looked like" Dahir but refuses to show a photo. Asks for ID and whether Dahir is a U.S. citizen.
- **Proof ignored** - Dahir shows passport card and driver's license. Officer takes both documents to their vehicle but does not release him.
- **Photographed** - Another officer photographs Dahir's face with a phone without consent. Friend also photographed and asked to show passport on phone.
- **Recording threatened** - Dahir starts recording. Officer tells him to stop; when he refuses, officer says: "We could detain you, wait, put the phone down."
- **Held in cold** - Officers hold Dahir for 25-30 minutes in 19°F weather. He has no head covering; his ears become cold and stiff. Officers refuse to let him leave despite work obligations.
- **Media arrives** - A media person with a camera and neighborhood residents gather.
- **Released** - Officer says to others: "It's getting hot over here, let's go, let's go." They return Dahir's documents and leave quickly without apology or acknowledgment.

## Affected Individual Statement

### Ali Dahir
On why he was stopped:
> "I believe I was stopped and seized solely because of the color of my skin and my appearance, as it was clear that the ICE officers did not know who I was, did not present any warrant, and did not say why I was stopped and seized. The officer said that they were looking for someone who looked like me, but he never gave me any specific description. I believe that he was just looking for a Somali person."

On the impact:
> "I missed the pickup that I had at 2:30 p.m., and the reason my employer understood was because I was on the local news. I try not to go outside unless it is necessary because I understand that ICE officers are all over the city, in restaurants, supermarkets, shopping malls, gas stations, and schools."

On feeling unsafe:
> "I always thought that if something like this happened to me, showing my driver's license and my passport card would be more than enough to show that I am a citizen. But this didn't seem to matter to them. They never acknowledged my citizenship."

## Constitutional Concerns
- Officers never identified themselves or presented a warrant
- Stopped without reasonable suspicion beyond appearance
- Photographed without consent
- Passport card presented and ignored as proof of citizenship
- Threatened with detention for exercising right to record
- Dahir and friend both held despite neither being the person sought

## Legal Action
Ali Dahir filed a sworn declaration as part of **Hussen v. Noem** (Case 26-cv-00324-ECT-ECW), the ACLU class-action lawsuit alleging racial profiling by ICE/CBP agents during Operation Metro Surge.

## Location Context
Dahir's apartment is on Lake Street near Karmel Mall, both major hubs for Somali people in Minneapolis. The apartment building's residents are mostly Somali and Latino.

## Editorial Assessment
**HIGH** - Sworn court declaration under penalty of perjury with detailed account. Video evidence filed with the court (Exhibit A on USB). Part of federal class-action lawsuit. Incident occurred near Karmel Mall, where other documented racial profiling incidents have taken place.
