# Citizen Check: U.S. Citizen Detained 30 Minutes Outside Apartment Despite Showing Passport

## Summary
Ali Dahir, a 44-year-old Somali American U.S. citizen, was stopped by five ICE agents outside his Lake Street apartment building on December 2, 2025. Despite presenting his passport card and Minnesota driver's license, agents held him for 25-30 minutes in 19-degree weather before leaving when media and neighbors gathered.
