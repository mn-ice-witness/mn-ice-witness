# ICE Vehicle Strikes Protester; Agent Laughs "Wipe Out!"

## Summary
An ICE vehicle struck a protester in Minneapolis' Cedar-Riverside neighborhood during a confrontation between federal agents and community members. Video captured by journalist Jon Farina shows the collision and an ICE agent laughing "Hahaha! Wipe out!" afterward. The incident occurred during the same enforcement action that led to the wrongful detention of U.S. citizen Mubashir.
