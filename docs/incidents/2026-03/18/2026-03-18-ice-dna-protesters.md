# NPR Investigation: ICE Collected DNA from Detained Protesters, Added to FBI Database

## Summary
An NPR investigation found that ICE officers collected DNA samples from people detained while observing immigration enforcement, adding the samples to the FBI's national CODIS database accessible by state and local law enforcement. In Minneapolis, a man identified as Ben was tackled by ICE agents in late January 2026 while filming in Northeast Minneapolis, sustaining three broken ribs and blunt chest trauma. Officers swabbed the inside of his cheek during his three-hour detention. NPR documented five additional similar cases across Minnesota, Illinois, and Oregon. Between 2020 and 2024, DHS collected DNA from more than 2,000 U.S. citizens.
