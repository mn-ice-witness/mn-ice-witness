# Noem Testifies 650 Agents Remain in Minnesota; Defends 'Domestic Terrorism' Claims; Fired Two Days Later

## Updates
- **Mar 5** - Trump [fires Noem as DHS Secretary](https://www.npr.org/2026/03/05/nx-s1-5667546/kristi-noem-homeland-security-fired) and reassigns her to a special envoy role. Sen. Markwayne Mullin nominated to replace her.

## Summary
DHS Secretary Kristi Noem testified before the Senate Judiciary Committee on March 3, confirming approximately 650 federal agents remain in Minnesota — roughly four times the 150-agent "regular footprint" that border czar Tom Homan promised on Feb. 20. Noem refused to apologize for characterizing slain citizens Renee Good and Alex Pretti as domestic terrorists, calling them "violent terrorists" who created "unprecedented" danger for agents. She was criticized by senators from both parties, with departing Sen. Thom Tillis (R-NC), who is not seeking re-election, comparing her leadership to the time she shot her own dog. Two days later, Trump fired Noem as DHS Secretary.
