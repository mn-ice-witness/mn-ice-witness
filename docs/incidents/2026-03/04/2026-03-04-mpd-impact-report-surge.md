# MPD Reports $5.2M Overtime in January, 50,000+ Calls, 9 Officers Injured During Metro Surge

## Summary
The Minneapolis Police Department presented an impact report to the City Council on March 4 documenting the toll of Operation Metro Surge (Dec 4, 2025 – Feb 15, 2026). MPD spent $5.2 million in overtime from January 7 to February 1 alone — double the amount budgeted for overtime for the entire year — while handling over 50,000 calls with 600 officers. Nine officers were injured, 14 filed PTSD cases, and 8 retired during the surge. The report documented 12 crashes between federal agents and the public, 2 fatal and 1 non-fatal shooting, and 7 chemical irritant deployments. Council members pressed Chief Brian O'Hara on why MPD didn't intervene against federal agents. O'Hara responded: "It is not possible, if federal law enforcement is present making arrests, for us to physically stop them from doing that."
