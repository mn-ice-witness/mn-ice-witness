# Trump-Appointed Judge Finds ICE Engaged in Unconstitutional Racial Profiling on at Least 23 Occasions

## Summary
In a 110-page ruling on the ACLU's Hussen v. Noem lawsuit, Trump-appointed U.S. District Judge Eric Tostrud found that ICE and CBP agents "likely engaged in unconstitutional policies" during Operation Metro Surge. Of 34 stops reviewed, only 5 had reasonable justification — at least 23 people were detained based solely on their race or ethnicity. Despite these findings, Tostrud declined to issue a preliminary injunction, citing the drawdown of agents. The case proceeds to trial.
